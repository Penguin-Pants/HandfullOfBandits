Insurgent Operations

Rally

+ Extort, Host Pressure or Strike Collaborators

Purpose: Augment forces, build Bases or flip guerillas underground

Location: any province without Displacement; Cities at Neutral/Opposition (Western Cities also at Support); Host/Tolerant camps; Europe. 
Cost: One resource per space selected

Procedure: Place 1 Underground Guerrilla; or replace 2 Guerrillas with 1 Base; or at a Base place up to Pop + Bases there;  or at a Base flip all Guerrillas Underground. 

Europe: Raise by 1; with Ban, cost 2 and max Europe 3.

Then: Agitate in one selected eligible space; pay extra.

March

+ Extort, Host Pressure or Strike Collaborators

Purpose: Move Guerrillas one adjacent step.


Location: Any province; No Western Cities, Europe or Iran. 


Procedure: Move each Guerrilla once. A group becomes Active if destination is at Support or it crosses an external border, and group size + State pieces at destination >3.

Cost: One resource per non-Mountain space selected


Restricted entry: +1 Resource per destination; always activate arrivals. Syria follows the chosen option.

Attack

+ Extort or Ambush

Location: Guerrillas and State pieces together.


Procedure: Record Troops present. Activate all Guerrillas. Roll ≤ Guerrillas: remove up to 2 State pieces; Troops/Police first, then Guards, then Bases.


Cost: One resource per space selected


Attrition: If Troops removed and same roll ≤ original Troops, remove 1 Guerrilla per Troop removed. Otherwise none. After attrition, a roll of 1 adds 1 Available Underground Guerrilla.

Ambush: Replaces this procedure in its chosen spaces.

Coerce

+ Extort or Strike Collaborators

Location: Domestic Neutral/Support space with an Underground Guerrilla and no Displacement.

Procedure: Activate 1 Guerrilla. If no Coercion is present, shift Support one level toward Neutral (no shift at Neutral), then add 1 Coercion. Existing Coercion blocks another marker and the shift; still activate.

Cost: One resource per space selected


Effect: Never creates Opposition. Coercion blocks political shifts and counts as PKK Harm.

Special Activities

Ambush


max 2 spaces

Purpose: Ensure attack success


Location:any spaces with an Underground Guerrilla


Procedure: In each, activate only 1 Underground Guerrilla; remove 1 State piece, Troops/Police first, then Guards, then Bases. No roll, no attrition, no bonus Guerrilla. Other Attack spaces resolve normally.



Extort



Purpose: Gain Resources.


Location: Any subset: domestic Pop ≥1, PKK Control and no Displacement; or Host/Tolerant camps. Each needs an Underground Guerrilla. No Europe/Iran.


Procedure: Activate 1 Underground Guerrilla in each selected location and gain 1 Resource per location.




Strike Collaborators


Before Rally / March / Coerce

Needs: Law 3175. Cost 1 per selected space.


Location: Up to 2 spaces, each with an Underground Guerrilla and a Guard.


Procedure: Remove up to 2 Guards total. Activate 1 Guerrilla and place 1 PKK Harm in each selected space. Europe −1 once for the action. No political shift. Guards return to State Available.

Harm: Blocks Opposition shifts until redressed at 2 each.




Host Pressure


2 · Rally / March

Location: One host box with a PKK piece; Syria also needs Leadership Present.


Procedure: Roll 1 die. Syria/PUK: 4–6 one step toward Host, 1 toward Hostile. KDP: 5–6 toward Host, 1–2 toward Hostile. Other rolls: no change.


Before-Op result: If posture makes the planned Op illegal/unaffordable, choose new legal locations for the same Op. Keep roll and SA cost; no reroll. If none qualify, Op ends with no locations.


Victory

Total Opposition + domestic PKK Bases ≥ 30 & Harm ≤ 6

Next-card order: only First Eligible’s choice matters. Op Only or Op + SA gives First Eligibility to the other faction. Event, Limited Op or Pass keeps the order.

Eligibility: PKK Base or PKK Control. ERNK: Rally-only City exception with a Guerrilla.

Costs: Remove Coercion 1 each; redress PKK Harm 2 each; shift toward Opposition 1 per level, max 2 shifts per space per action/phase. Clear all Coercion and PKK Harm before shifting. No Legitimacy cap.

ARGK Formed

(5.3)Once per game · Pivotal

Trigger: At least 8 domestic Guerrillas and 2 domestic PKK Bases. Play after Always, before First Eligible acts.

Effect: Cover Event; place up to 4 Guerrillas among Base spaces; flip all Guerrillas Underground in every Base space. Gain 3 Resources. PKK First Eligible in Event box; State Op + SA or Pass. PKK stays First next card.

Propaganda round (6.0)

1

Victory: skip round 1. Round 2: check score thresholds and Harm ≤6. If a faction wins, stop.

2

Income: round 1 only. State: 8 + floor(Legitimacy ÷ 2) + Pop of each Controlled Mountain/Plain with a State Base, counted once. PKK: domestic Bases + Host/Tolerant camp Bases + Leadership + Europe income.

3

Guard upkeep: State pays ceil(Guards ÷ 2). If unable, remove enough Guards to pay the reduced cost.

4

Political adjustment: apply the ordered list below. Legal Politics is inactive in C1/C2.

5

Civic Action: State spends first; Control + Troops + Police; total cap floor(current Legitimacy ÷ 2).

6

Agitation: PKK spends in Base/Controlled spaces. Round 2 ends here: reveal any Covert markers and score final margins; no Redeploy/Reset.

7

Redeploy: use pre-Redeploy Control/inventory. State first: Troops to domestic Cities/State Bases; all Iraqi Troops must leave. Police to previously Controlled spaces. PKK to adjacent Base spaces, subject to host access/costs.

8

Reset: remove 1 Coercion per space; all Guerrillas Underground; discard Momentum, keep Capabilities; remove Restraint Broken; advance Campaign; PKK First Eligible.

Political adjustment order (6.4)

1. Emergency drain: Martial Law −1, OHAL −1, Extended Powers −2, Normalizing 0 Legitimacy. 2. Lose 1 Legitimacy per New Displacement; flip to Old. 3. If ≥3 Reception, lose 1 Legitimacy. 4. If ≥3 Disputed Harm, lose 1 Legitimacy. 5. If ≥3 face-down Covert, randomly reveal one: State Harm and −2 Legitimacy. 6. If State Harm exceeds PKK Harm by ≥3, Europe +1; if reverse, Europe −1. Then Europe 4–5 costs 1 Legitimacy. 7. Gain 1 Legitimacy if no New Displacement flipped. 8. May move OHAL → Normalizing, Normalizing → OHAL (−1 Legitimacy) or Extended Powers → OHAL. Other voluntary moves unavailable in Scenario A.

Legitimacy 0–20. Each unpaid loss below 0 costs 2 State Resources. Europe 0–5: income 0 at 0–1, 1 at 2–3, 2 at 4–5. Leadership Present income: Syria Host 2, Tolerant 1; otherwise 0.
