# 1.0 Introduction

*A Handful of Bandits* is a two-player research prototype about the conflict in southeastern Turkey. One player directs the **State** and the other the **PKK**. The State seeks Support and Legitimacy. The PKK seeks Opposition and domestic Bases. Military Control, political attitudes and national Legitimacy are separate measures.

This edition covers the **full game, 1984–1999**: five Campaigns (C1 to C5). The **full campaign** plays all five Campaigns and is the only scenario in this edition. Section 10 gives its setup. The shorter Scenarios A, B and C are archived (10.6). The edition includes the designer decisions implemented in ruleset R4. It is not a balanced or final published game.

**Reading the rules.** Numbered rules give the procedures. Italic examples explain them. Card text can modify a procedure (5.1). Section 10 gives the complete setups. Appendix A reproduces the Event text for Campaigns C1 and C2 only. The text of the C3 to C5 cards is not yet reproduced; read it in the simulator or in the card package. Appendix B identifies implementation differences and systems outside this edition.

**Campaign gates.** Some systems start in a later Campaign: Village Clearance, Covert Action, Declare Ceasefire and Legal Politics from C3, and Return by Civic Action in C5 (10.8). A rule that names a Campaign applies only from that Campaign.

> **DESIGN NOTE:** Pieces, Population (Pop), Resources and scores are design weights. They are not counts of people, measures of ethnic preference or historical estimates of force size. No rule awards points for casualties or displacement.

## 1.1 General Course of Play

Reveal one card at a time. On an Event card, First Eligible chooses an action. Second Eligible then responds within the options that the first choice permits. Adjust initiative and reveal the next card. A Propaganda card interrupts card play for income, upkeep, political spending and other adjustments (6.0).

There is **no next-card preview**. A faction may remain First Eligible for several cards. Passing does not automatically give priority to the passer (2.4).

## 1.3 The Map

**1.3.1 Domestic spaces.** Each domestic space has a terrain type and Pop. Only domestic spaces contribute Support, Opposition and Bases to victory scores. Pop remains unchanged by displacement.

| Space | Terrain | Pop [design] |
|:---|:---|---:|
| Hakkari | Mountain | 1 |
| Botan | Mountain | 2 |
| Mardin-Batman | Plain | 4 |
| Diyarbakır City (Amed) | City | 2 |
| Diyarbakır Rural | Plain | 2 |
| Şanlıurfa | Plain | 4 |
| Adıyaman | Plain | 2 |
| Elazığ | Plain | 2 |
| Dersim (Tunceli) | Mountain | 1 |
| Bingöl-Muş | Mountain | 3 |

**1.3.2 Adjacency.** A shared gameplay border or listed link determines adjacency. Decorative rivers and relief have no additional rule effect. Diyarbakır City touches only Diyarbakır Rural. Section 10.4 lists all movement links.

**1.3.3 External boxes.** Syria-Bekaa, KDP Zone, PUK Zone, Iran and Europe are external. They have no Support, Opposition or Control and do not contribute Bases to the PKK victory score. Syria-Bekaa, KDP and PUK hold camps subject to host posture (8.1). KDP and PUK are Mountain for combat. Iran is Event-only. Europe holds a track and **no pieces**.

**1.3.4 Western Cities.** This domestic City is an abstract political node with no land adjacency. No Garrison, Sweep or March enters or leaves it. State pieces can enter or leave by Air Mobility or Redeploy, or be placed by Train or Events. The PKK can Rally there; its Guerrillas cannot March out. No PKK Base or Village Guard may be placed there.

## 1.4 Pieces and Supply

**1.4.1 Placement and removal.** Place pieces from Available. Removed pieces return to Available unless the card says otherwise. Newly placed Guerrillas are Underground. Bases never move. Guards never move. There is no Casualties box.

**1.4.2 Finite supply.** An ordinary Operation must meet its placement and stacking requirements. An Event does as much as possible with the available supply (5.1). Rally is the one exception: it places as many Guerrillas as are Available (3.3.1). The simulator does not provide voluntary on-map piece withdrawal to replenish supply; see Appendix B for the difference from the wider research draft.

**1.4.3 Stacking.** Each domestic space may hold at most two Bases of each faction. Each external camp box may hold at most two PKK Bases. No PKK Base in Western Cities; no State Base in an external box. A Mountain or Plain may hold at most three Guards. Troops, Police and Guerrillas have no general stacking limit.

**1.4.4 Foreign access.** Only PKK pieces may occupy Syria-Bekaa or Iran. State Troops enter KDP or PUK only through Cross-Border. No Police, Guards or State Bases enter an external box. Markers do not count for stacking or Control.

## 1.5 Control

A faction Controls a domestic space when its pieces there outnumber the other faction's pieces. Count Troops, Police, Guards and State Bases for the State; count Guerrillas of both states and PKK Bases for the PKK. A tie gives neither faction Control. Markers do not count.

Recompute Control when pieces change, except during Redeploy, which uses the position before either faction moves (6.8). Control gives no victory points by itself and does not change Support or Opposition.

> **EXAMPLE:** Botan starts with two Troops and one Police against two Guerrillas and one PKK Base. The piece totals are 3–3, so neither side Controls it. Its Passive Opposition remains in place.

## 1.6 Support and Opposition

Each domestic space is at one of five levels, in this order:

**Active Opposition → Passive Opposition → Neutral → Passive Support → Active Support.**

A shift moves one level. A shift toward Support from Passive Opposition first reaches Neutral. Neutral has no marker. Passive Support contributes Pop to Total Support; Active Support contributes twice Pop. Opposition scores the same way for Total Opposition.

Coercion blocks shifts in either direction. State Harm blocks shifts toward Support. PKK Harm blocks shifts toward Opposition. Reception also blocks Support shifts until relieved. Remove the relevant blocks before paying for a shift (3.6). An Office caps its space at Passive Support and Passive Opposition: no shift takes the space past those levels (8.4).

## 1.7 Resources, Legitimacy and Europe

**Resources** range from 0 to 50 for each faction. Pay the full required cost before taking a voluntary action. A forced loss stops at zero; unused excess is lost. Gains stop at 50.

**Legitimacy** ranges from 0 to 20 and represents acceptance of State conduct. It affects the State score, income and Propaganda Civic Action cap. If a Legitimacy loss would go below zero, lose two State Resources per unpaid Legitimacy point. Any remaining unpaid loss is ignored.

**Europe** ranges from 0 to 5. It represents diaspora resources and international pressure. At Propaganda it gives PKK income of 0 at Europe 0–1, 1 at Europe 2–3 and 2 at Europe 4–5. At 4–5 it also costs one Legitimacy in the political adjustment step. While the Europe Ban marker is on the map, Europe cannot exceed 3. Only the card DK-064 places it. Nothing removes it, so it stays for the rest of the game. It is not the Legal Politics ban (8.5).

## 1.8 Harm

**State Harm count** equals State Harm markers on the map. **PKK Harm count** equals PKK Harm markers plus Coercion markers on the map. Count qualifying markers in external boxes too. Disputed Harm belongs to neither faction. Face-down Covert markers count as State Harm at each victory check (7.2) and become State Harm when revealed (8.4). Harm affects victory eligibility and final margins (7.0).

Harm is not removed automatically. Civic Action can redress State Harm; Agitation can redress PKK Harm. Reset removes one Coercion per space. Events may remove or alter markers as printed.

# 2.0 Sequence of Play

## 2.1 Prepare the Game

Follow 10.0. Set PKK First Eligible and State Second Eligible. Build the piles of the five Campaigns separately (10.3), then stack them in Campaign order. Give each faction its Pivotals (5.3, 10.1); they are held in hand and are not shuffled into the deck.

## 2.2 Reveal a Card

Reveal the top card of the first pile with cards remaining. Apply any **Always** instruction immediately. If it is Propaganda, conduct 6.0. Otherwise offer the Pivotal window (5.3), then First Eligible acts.

Played cards and the count of cards left may be inspected. Unused pool cards and unrevealed cards remain unseen. Cards below a Propaganda card remain in the deck and are played after that round, in the new Campaign.

## 2.3 Choices on an Event Card

First Eligible selects one box and resolves its choice completely:

- **Event:** execute either side of the current card (5.0).
- **Limited Op:** one Operation in one location, without a Special Activity.
- **Op Only:** one Operation without a Special Activity, in two or more locations. An Operation in one location without a Special Activity is a Limited Op.
- **Op + SA:** one Operation with one permitted Special Activity.
- **Pass:** take no Operation or Event; gain Pass Resources (2.5).

From C3, the PKK may use the Limited Op box to **Declare Ceasefire** (8.6). It takes the place of the Operation and is legal wherever a Limited Op is legal.

Place that faction's cylinder in the selected box. Second Eligible then chooses from this matrix:

| First chose | Second may choose |
|:---|:---|
| Event | Op + SA or Pass |
| Limited Op | Op Only, Op + SA or Pass |
| Op Only | Limited Op or Pass |
| Op + SA | Event, Limited Op or Pass |
| Pass | Any of the five boxes |

After both factions finish, adjust eligibility (2.4), clear the boxes and reveal the next card. The responder cannot execute the same Event after First Eligible takes it. Pass is a distinct choice: only Pass gives Pass Resources. A faction may take the Event box and apply none of its text. It then keeps First Eligible (2.4).

## 2.4 Next-Card Eligibility

**Only the First Eligible faction's choice sets next-card order.** Second Eligible's choice does not change that order.

| First Eligible chose | Next card's First Eligible |
|:---|:---|
| Op Only or Op + SA | The other faction |
| Event, Limited Op or Pass | The same faction |

If both Pass, keep the order. A Pivotal player becomes First Eligible (5.3). Reset separately restores PKK First Eligible (6.9).

> **EXAMPLE:** State is First Eligible, takes an Event and PKK Passes. State remains First Eligible next card. If State instead takes Op Only, PKK becomes First Eligible even if PKK Passes. Passing does not itself promote the passer.

## 2.5 Passing

A passing State gains **2 Resources**; a passing PKK gains **1 Resource**, to the normal limit of 50. It takes no other action on that card. The other faction still receives its response. Pass does not defer a choice to a later point on the same card.

## 2.6 Limited Operations

A Limited Op selects one location, which may be a domestic space, a permitted external box or Europe for Rally. No Special Activity accompanies it. Garrison, Sweep and March may collect pieces from several origins into the one permitted destination. Train Civic Action and Rally Agitate remain permitted within that one location, at their normal extra cost.

## 2.7 Timing and Randomness

In this playable prototype, a Special Activity resolves **before or after** the Operation, not interleaved during it. Cross-Border and Ambush use before timing. Strike Collaborators, Village Clearance and Covert Action may go before or after the Operation, but each needs its conditions to hold on the board at that moment. Conditions and payment are checked on the board each part meets. An after-Operation SA that no longer meets its conditions is skipped.

Use a six-sided die for rolls. Apply printed modifiers without treating natural 1 or 6 as an automatic failure or success. Track changes stop at their endpoints, except Legitimacy overflow (1.7).

The simulator uses a stored random seed. The same scenario, options and seed reproduce the same deck and random sequence. Home starts with seed 1. Select **Random** before Start for a new seed; a different seed can still give the same first card by chance.

# 3.0 Operations

## 3.1 General Procedure

Choose one Operation type and its locations. Pay its cost for each selected location unless it has a total cost. A piece cannot move more than once during the same movement Operation. Operations must satisfy their conditions and use only available pieces. A Special Activity has its own cost and limits (4.0).

**Mountains:** Sweep and Garrison activate one Underground Guerrilla per two eligible State pieces, rounded down. Assault removes one PKK piece per two Troops, rounded down. KDP and PUK count as Mountains. Guards assist detection, not Assault. In Cities, Police assist Assault.

## 3.2 State Operations

### 3.2.1 Train

**Cost:** 2 Resources per selected domestic space. Choose one option in each:

- **Recruit regular forces:** in a City or a space with a State Base, place one to three Troops and Police in any mix from Available.
- **Recruit Guards:** after Law 3175, in a State-Controlled Mountain or Plain with Police, place one or two Guards from Available, to the three-Guard limit. Tribal Allies removes only the Police requirement (5.2).
- **Build a Base:** in a State-Controlled Mountain or Plain with at least two Police and room for another State Base, replace two Police with one Available State Base.

After placement, the State may conduct Civic Action in **one selected space** (3.6). It needs State Control and Police there. No Troop is required for Train Civic Action. Pay Civic Action costs even if the Train is free.

### 3.2.2 Garrison

**Cost:** 2 Resources total.

Move up to six Police among domestic spaces, excluding movement into or out of Western Cities. Then select one space with Police and activate Underground Guerrillas there, up to the number of Police plus Guards. In a Mountain, halve that number and round down. Count pieces after movement. Troops do not contribute to Garrison activation.

For a Limited Op, all moved Police go to one destination and activation occurs there. Garrison does not move Guards or Troops.

### 3.2.3 Sweep

**Cost:** 2 Resources per selected space; 1 at OHAL Extended Powers.

Select domestic spaces other than Western Cities. Move any Troops from adjacent spaces into the selected spaces. Each Troop moves one step only. Police and Guards stay in place. Then activate Underground Guerrillas in each selected space up to the number of Troops, Police and Guards there, halved and rounded down in Mountains.

Use Cross-Border to Sweep a permitted Iraqi zone (4.2.2). For a Limited Op, select one destination; Troops may come from several adjacent spaces.

> **EXAMPLE:** Botan has four Troops, one Police and two Guards. A Sweep can activate up to three Underground Guerrillas: floor((4 + 1 + 2) ÷ 2). It cannot then Assault as part of the same Operation.

### 3.2.4 Assault

**Cost:** 2 Resources per selected space.

Use Troops to remove PKK pieces. Remove one per Troop, or one per two Troops in a Mountain, rounded down. In a City, Police count as Troops. Guards do not contribute. Use Cross-Border for Assault in KDP or PUK.

Remove **Active Guerrillas first**. A PKK Base may be removed only when no Guerrilla of either state remains in that space. Never remove an Underground Guerrilla by Assault. Unused removal capacity is lost. Removed pieces return to PKK Available. Removing a Base gives no State score or Legitimacy award.

## 3.3 PKK Operations

### 3.3.1 Rally

**Cost:** 1 Resource per selected location.

A domestic space must have no Displacement marker. A City must be Neutral or Opposition, except Western Cities may also be selected at Support. A host box must permit Rally (8.1). Iran cannot Rally. Europe is a separate Rally location.

Choose one option in each selected space or camp:

- Place one Available Guerrilla Underground.
- Replace two Guerrillas with one Available PKK Base, subject to stacking and host limits. No Base in Western Cities. The prototype removes Active Guerrillas first for this replacement.
- At a PKK Base, place up to **Pop + PKK Bases there** Guerrillas from Available. An external box counts as Pop 1. This is a placement allowance, not a ceiling on the total Guerrillas present.
- At a PKK Base, flip all Guerrillas there Underground.

If a Rally plans more Guerrillas than are Available, place all that are Available.

In **Europe**, pay the Rally cost and raise Europe by one instead of placing pieces. If the Europe Ban marker is present, Europe Rally costs 2 and Europe cannot exceed 3.

After Rally placement, the PKK may Agitate in one selected space with a PKK Base or PKK Control (3.6). Pay normal Agitation costs even if Rally is free. ERNK, Academy and Momentum may modify these options (5.2).

### 3.3.2 March

**Cost:** 1 Resource per destination; add 1 for a Restricted host destination.

Move Guerrillas one step from adjacent spaces or camp boxes into each selected destination. A moving piece can move only once. No March enters or leaves Western Cities. No March enters Europe or Iran. Leaving a camp is allowed even if its posture is closed to entry.

A **group** is all Guerrillas that move from one origin to one destination in this March. Two origins that enter one destination are two groups. A moving group becomes Active if both conditions hold:

- Its destination is at Support, or it crosses into or out of an external box; and
- Its group size plus State pieces in the destination is **more than three**.

Entry into a Restricted box always activates the entering group. Hostile or Aligned boxes are closed to entry. Syria at Restricted follows the selected prototype option (8.1.3). A Limited March has one destination and may use several adjacent origins.

### 3.3.3 Attack

**Cost:** 1 Resource per selected space with Guerrillas and State pieces.

Before resolving the Attack, count the Troops in the space. Activate all Guerrillas there and roll one die. If the result is **less than or equal to** the number of Guerrillas, the Attack succeeds. Remove up to two State pieces: choose Troops or Police first, then Guards, then State Bases.

**Attrition (DD-21):** if the Attack removes Troops, compare the **same die result** with the Troop count recorded before the Attack. If the result is **less than or equal to** that count, remove one Guerrilla for each Troop removed. If the result is **greater than** that count, remove no Guerrillas for attrition. If no Troops are removed, no attrition occurs. Return removed pieces to Available. Do not roll again.

After attrition, a roll of 1 also places one Available Underground Guerrilla.

> **EXAMPLE:** Four Guerrillas attack two Troops. A roll of 1–4 succeeds. If both Troops are removed, a roll of 1–2 also removes two Guerrillas; a roll of 3–4 causes no attrition. On a roll of 1, place the bonus Underground Guerrilla afterward.

**Ambush replaces this procedure in its selected spaces:** no roll, activate one Underground Guerrilla, remove one State piece, no attrition and no bonus Guerrilla (4.3.1). Other Attack spaces resolve normally.

### 3.3.4 Coerce

**Cost:** 1 Resource per selected space; **2 if Pop is 3 or more**.

Select domestic spaces at Neutral or Support with an Underground Guerrilla and no Displacement. Activate one Guerrilla in each. If no Coercion marker is already there, a space at Support shifts one level toward Neutral, then receives one Coercion. A Neutral space receives the marker without shifting.

An existing Coercion marker prevents an additional marker and blocks the shift; activation still occurs. A PKK Harm marker in the space also blocks the shift toward Neutral (1.6). The Coercion marker is still placed. Coerce never shifts a space toward Opposition. Coercion counts against the PKK Harm gate until removed.

## 3.4 Movement Restrictions

Guards and Bases never move. Air Mobility never takes Troops into or out of an external box. Troops in Iraqi zones remain until Redeploy unless a specific Event removes them. Garrison and Sweep cannot connect Western Cities to the regional map. A river drawing does not create an extra movement link or cost.

## 3.5 Detection Before Destruction

Underground Guerrillas cannot be removed by ordinary Assault or Cross-Border Assault. Sweep or Garrison can activate them first. PKK actions also expose Guerrillas as specified. A specific Event can override ordinary removal restrictions when its text says so.

## 3.6 Civic Action and Agitation

**3.6.1 Civic Action.** The State needs Control and at least one Police. During Propaganda it also needs at least one Troop. Pay for any combination of these steps, clearing all relevant blocks before shifting:

| Civic Action step | Cost |
|:---|---:|
| Remove one Coercion | 1 |
| Relief: remove one Reception | 1 |
| Redress: remove one State Harm | 3 |
| Return: remove one Displacement | 3 |
| Shift one level toward Support | 2 |

Maximum two Support shifts per space per action or phase. Return is permitted at Normalizing, in C5 or by an applicable Event; it does not itself remove State Harm or grant Support. The State's **total** Propaganda Civic Action spending may not exceed half current Legitimacy, rounded down. There is no such cap on Train Civic Action.

**3.6.2 Agitation.** The PKK needs a **PKK Base or PKK Control**, both during Rally and during Propaganda. ERNK gives a Rally-only City exception (5.2). Pay any combination:

| Agitation step | Cost |
|:---|---:|
| Remove one Coercion | 1 |
| Redress: remove one PKK Harm | 2 |
| Shift one level toward Opposition | 1 |

Maximum two Opposition shifts per space per action or phase. Remove every Coercion and PKK Harm marker that blocks the planned shifts. Agitation has no Legitimacy-based spending cap. It is limited by PKK Resources. In an external Base space it can remove qualifying markers, but cannot shift a political level because the box has none.

> **EXAMPLE:** Botan has a PKK Base, one Coercion and one PKK Harm. One Opposition shift costs 4 Resources: 1 to remove Coercion, 2 to redress PKK Harm and 1 to shift. The PKK may instead pay only for removals and make no shift.

# 4.0 Special Activities

## 4.1 General Rules

Take at most one Special Activity with an Op + SA choice. It may accompany only the Operations listed for it. No SA accompanies Op Only or Limited Op. Its spaces may differ from the Operation unless its rule requires an overlap. Pay its cost in addition to the Operation cost. Timing is before or after, subject to 2.7.

## 4.2 State Special Activities

### 4.2.1 Air Mobility

**Accompanies:** any State Op. **Cost:** free.

Move up to four Troops among up to three domestic spaces. Count **origins and destinations** toward the three-space limit. Western Cities is allowed. External boxes are never allowed, even as origins. Strike Force raises the limits to six Troops among four spaces (5.2).

### 4.2.2 Cross-Border

**Accompanies:** Sweep or Assault. **Timing:** before. **Cost:** 2 extra Resources, or zero extra if the target host is Aligned with Turkey.

Choose one zone, also selected for the Operation:

- **KDP:** requires KDP Restricted, Hostile or Aligned. After the Northern Iraq Vacuum card (5.4), KDP Tolerant also qualifies.
- **PUK:** requires both PUK and KDP to qualify in the same way.
- **Early hot pursuit:** in C1 and C2, with the option allowed, KDP may be entered at any posture, but the total Troops there after the move may not exceed three. No such exception opens PUK. The exception ends when C3 begins (10.8).

Move up to six Troops from Hakkari and/or Botan into the target zone. PUK movement may pass through KDP as part of this lift. Conduct the accompanying Sweep or Assault in the zone as one selected location. It is Mountain terrain. If its host is Aligned, ignore the Mountain divisor for Assault.

If Cross-Border Assault removes any PKK piece and the zone has no Disputed Harm, place one Disputed Harm marker there. Troops remain in the zone until Redeploy and give no Control. Never Cross-Border into Syria-Bekaa or Iran.

### 4.2.3 Diplomacy

**Accompanies:** any State Op. **Cost:** 2 Resources.

Target one of Syria, KDP, PUK or Europe. Roll one die, adding or subtracting:

- +1 at Legitimacy 12 or more; −1 at Legitimacy 5 or less.
- −1 for Syria in C1 to C4. The penalty ends in C5.
- +1 for KDP from C3.
- Any bonus printed by the granting Event.

A modified result of 5 or more moves the host one step toward Hostile, or lowers Europe by one. Moving a host from Hostile to Aligned requires 6 or more. Syria has no Aligned step; a further move at Hostile has no posture effect. A posture change may activate Guerrillas and flip the Leadership Abroad (8.1).

> **EXAMPLE:** At Legitimacy 12, Diplomacy against Syria in C2 has +1 for Legitimacy and −1 for early Syria: net zero. A die roll of 5 moves Syria one step toward Hostile. A natural 6 does not bypass the modified-total requirement for another target.

### 4.2.4 Village Clearance

**Accompanies:** Sweep or Garrison. **Cost:** Legitimacy (see below). **Available:** from C3.

Village Clearance is a forced evacuation of a village area. All of these conditions must hold:

1. Emergency Rule is OHAL or OHAL Extended Powers.
2. The Campaign is C3 or later.
3. The target is one Mountain or Plain space that the Operation selected. It has State Control, at least one Troop and at least one Police, is at Neutral or Opposition and has no Displacement marker.
4. Legitimacy is at least (2 × the target's Pop) + 1.

Apply these effects in this order:

1. Shift the space one level toward Neutral. An existing State Harm or Coercion marker blocks this shift; the other effects still apply.
2. Lose Legitimacy equal to 2 × the target's Pop.
3. Place a Displacement marker on its New side.
4. Place a State Harm marker.
5. Place a Reception marker in Diyarbakır City or Western Cities (State choice). If no Reception marker is available, lose one Legitimacy instead.
6. Activate all Guerrillas in the space.
7. If the space holds an Office, lose one more Legitimacy.

The space keeps its Pop. A New Displacement marker costs one Legitimacy at the next Propaganda round (6.4) and blocks PKK Rally, Extort and Coerce there until Return (3.6.1). The EU-Turkey Customs Union Momentum can raise the cost: Village Clearance then costs one more Legitimacy in effect 2, and condition 4 needs (2 × Pop) + 2.

### 4.2.5 Covert Action

**Accompanies:** Garrison or Sweep. **Cost:** none now; Legitimacy later. **Available:** from C3, at Legitimacy 4 or more.

Covert Action is the covert killing of insurgents. Choose one space with State Control, at least one Police and at least one Underground Guerrilla. The Operation need not have selected this space. The space must have no Office, and a Covert marker must be available.

Remove one Underground Guerrilla without Activation. Place one **face-down Covert marker** in the space. Both players see the placement.

When a Covert marker is revealed, by an Event, by Propaganda exposure (6.4) or by final scoring (6.7), it becomes a State Harm marker and the State loses two Legitimacy. Each Covert marker still face down costs the State two points at every victory check (7.2).

## 4.3 PKK Special Activities

### 4.3.1 Ambush

**Accompanies:** Attack. **Timing:** before. **Cost:** free.

Choose up to two Attack spaces, each with an Underground Guerrilla. In each, **do not roll**. Activate exactly one Underground Guerrilla and remove one State piece in the Attack removal order: Troops or Police first, then Guards, then State Bases. No Guerrilla is lost to attrition and no roll-of-1 bonus is received. Other Guerrillas remain as they were.

Ambush Momentum increases the limit to three spaces. The no-roll procedure still applies in every Ambush space.

### 4.3.2 Extort

**Accompanies:** any PKK Op. **Cost:** free.

Choose any subset of qualifying locations. In each, activate one Underground Guerrilla and gain one Resource. A domestic space needs Pop of at least one, PKK Control and no Displacement. An external camp needs Host or Tolerant posture and an Underground Guerrilla. Europe and Iran do not qualify as host camps.

### 4.3.3 Strike Collaborators

**Accompanies:** Rally, March or Coerce. **Timing:** before or after. **Needs:** Law 3175. **Cost:** 1 Resource per selected space.

Select up to two spaces, each with an Underground Guerrilla and at least one Guard. Remove up to two Guards **in total**, activate one Guerrilla in each selected space and place one PKK Harm in each. Lower Europe by one, once for the action.

This SA gives no Support/Opposition shift. The resulting PKK Harm blocks subsequent Agitation shifts there until the PKK pays 2 per marker to redress it. A removed Guard returns to State Available and never becomes a PKK piece.

### 4.3.4 Host Pressure

**Accompanies:** Rally or March. **Cost:** 2 Resources.

Select one host box with a PKK piece. Syria also requires Leadership Present. Roll one die:

| Host | Toward Host | Toward Hostile | No change |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Syria or PUK | 4–6 | 1 | 2–3 |
| KDP | 5–6 | 1–2 | 3–4 |

Move one posture step in the indicated direction. If before-Operation Host Pressure makes the planned Rally locations or March destinations illegal or unaffordable, keep the roll and paid SA cost. Choose new legal locations for **the same Operation**. Do not reroll or pay for Host Pressure again. In the simulator, this is a continuation of the saved action, not a second action. If no legal, affordable replacement exists, the Operation ends with no locations: the roll, the posture change and the paid cost stand.

# 5.0 Events

## 5.1 Executing Events

The executing faction chooses the unshaded or shaded side, not both. Either faction may choose either side unless the card expressly restricts it. The executing faction makes choices that the card does not assign to someone else. A named chooser in card text, such as "State choice", names the normal executor of that side; the executing faction makes the choice.

**PKK restriction.** The PKK may not execute an Event side that places or resolves a Closure Case, bans the legal party, removes Offices or moves Legal Space. The non-player Office sides of DK-065, DK-067 and DK-094, and both sides of DK-053, stay open to either faction. They run a fixed procedure and give no choice of target.

Card text overrides an ordinary rule when it says to do so. It does not waive stacking, finite piece supply or track endpoints unless explicitly permitted. Do as much as possible. If a piece or marker is unavailable, skip that placement and record it. Marker exhaustion does not create a substitute marker; Harm exhaustion is a playtest warning.

An Event may grant a free Operation or SA. Its ordinary Resource cost is waived, but its location limits, required targets and other conditions still apply unless overridden. Civic Action, Agitation and Redress retain their normal extra costs. A free action does not move an initiative cylinder separately; the acting faction remains in the Event box for its card choice.

**Always instructions** apply on reveal even if nobody executes the Event, or a Pivotal covers it. An ordinary Event's optional text does not apply merely because the card is revealed.

## 5.2 Capabilities and Momentum

A Capability lasts for the game. Momentum lasts until the next Reset. Keep the selected side visible. The current Event takes precedence over a conflicting lasting effect. Cards from C3 to C5 print their own lasting effects: Capability DK-091 in C5 and Momentum DK-038, DK-050, DK-072 and DK-074 in C3 and C4. The C1 and C2 lasting effects are:

- **ERNK (DK-004 shaded, Capability):** Rally Agitate may select a City with a PKK Guerrilla even without a PKK Base or PKK Control. It does not change Propaganda Agitation eligibility.
- **Tribal Allies (DK-006 unshaded, Capability):** Train may recruit Guards in a State-Controlled Mountain or Plain without Police. Law 3175 and all other limits still apply.
- **Extra Agitate (DK-007 shaded, Momentum):** Rally may Agitate in one extra selected space, at normal cost. Do not spend twice in the same space as separate Agitations.
- **Academy (DK-009 shaded, Capability):** while Syria is Host or Tolerant, Rally in Syria-Bekaa may place up to two extra Guerrillas in addition to its normal recruitment allowance.
- **Rivals (DK-010 unshaded, Momentum):** a Rally recruitment at a Mountain PKK Base places one fewer Guerrilla, minimum one.
- **Ambush (DK-011 shaded, Momentum):** Ambush may select up to three Attack spaces.
- **Strike Force (DK-022 unshaded, Capability):** Air Mobility may move up to six Troops among up to four domestic spaces.

A Capability card's other side may be a one-time Event. Taking that other side does not install the lasting effect printed on its opposite side.

**Window.** After an Event card is revealed and its Always line is applied, but before First Eligible chooses, a faction may play one Pivotal whose Campaign has been reached and whose condition holds. Second Eligible has priority if both factions could play one. A faction that holds two eligible Pivotals chooses one; only one Pivotal may be played on a card. Declining keeps the card for a later window. The Pivotal replaces the revealed Event, which cannot then be executed.

**Serhildan.** Shift up to three domestic spaces that hold a PKK piece and no Office one level toward Opposition. Diyarbakır City may be one. Europe +1 (the Ban cap applies). The normal shift rule applies: a PKK Harm or Coercion marker blocks a shift toward Opposition.

**Coercive Diplomacy on Syria.** Move Syria to Hostile with the normal Hostile entry: Guerrillas in Syria-Bekaa Activate and the Leadership goes Abroad (8.1). PKK pieces there stay but earn no income.

## 5.5 Guard Abuse and Attribution

When Guard Abuse is executed, in **every qualifying space** with a Guard and any Harm marker, lose one Legitimacy and remove one Guard. Apply once per qualifying space. Coercion alone is not a Harm marker for this instruction, although it counts toward PKK Harm for victory.

Use the Harm attribution printed on the card. Disputed Harm remains Disputed unless a specific rule changes it. Do not assign it to a faction by inference from a card title or historical assumption. A later Event may turn a Disputed marker into State Harm only if the card rests on a High or Moderate court or commission finding.

## 5.6 Kurdish Hizbullah and Covert Violence

Event cards, not players, represent Kurdish Hizbullah and unattributed killings. No player owns or orders them, and card titles stay neutral. When the card DK-053 runs its procedure: if any of Botan, Mardin-Batman, Diyarbakır City and Diyarbakır Rural holds an Office, remove one Office from the highest-Pop such space. Otherwise remove one Guerrilla, Underground first, from the highest-Pop such space that holds a Guerrilla. Break ties in this order: Mardin-Batman, Diyarbakır City, Diyarbakır Rural, Botan. The executing faction does not choose the target. Place a Disputed Harm marker there. The Event does nothing if there is no target.

Cards that reveal Covert markers reveal them as 4.2.5 describes. A card that says "reveal up to two" lets the executor stop after one.

# 6.0 Propaganda Rounds

Conduct these steps in order when Propaganda is revealed. Skip an immediately consecutive Propaganda card without conducting another round. The **first** Propaganda round of a game skips the victory check. The **final** round is round 5. It gives no income and ends after Agitation unless a faction already wins at the opening check.

## 6.1 Victory Phase

Skip in round one. In every later round, check both scores and Harm gates as in 7.2. If a winner qualifies, end the game immediately. Do not then pay income, upkeep or conduct political spending.

## 6.2 Resources Phase

**Skip income in the final round.** In a non-final round:

**State income:** 8 + half Legitimacy, rounded down + the Pop of each State-Controlled Mountain or Plain that contains a State Base. Count the space's Pop once, regardless of its Base count. Cities do not add this income term.

**PKK income:** one per domestic PKK Base + one per PKK Base in a Host or Tolerant camp + Leadership income + Europe income. Leadership Present gives 2 if Syria is Host, 1 if Tolerant and 0 otherwise. Abroad gives none. After Capture (8.1.5) subtract one more. Europe gives 0/1/2 as in 1.7. The PKK total is never below 0. Each faction stops at 50 Resources.

> **EXAMPLE:** On the starting board (10.2) the State's income is 19 and PKK income is 5. Starting Resources of 12 and 5 would become 31 and 10 in a non-final round before upkeep. No such addition occurs in a final round.

## 6.3 Guard Upkeep

The State pays one Resource per two Guards on the map, **rounded up**. If it cannot pay, remove Guards of its choice until it can pay for those remaining, then pay that reduced upkeep. If it can pay the full amount, it must pay; it cannot dismiss Guards voluntarily in this step.

## 6.4 Legitimacy and Emergency Rule

Apply the following in order, with the normal Legitimacy bounds and overflow rule:

1. **Emergency drain:** Martial Law −1; OHAL −1; OHAL Extended Powers −2; Normalizing 0.
2. **Displacement:** lose one Legitimacy per New Displacement and flip each New marker to Old.
3. **Reception:** lose one Legitimacy if three or more Reception markers are on the map.
4. **Disputed Harm:** lose one Legitimacy if three or more Disputed Harm markers are on the map.
5. **Covert exposure:** if at least three face-down Covert markers are on the map, randomly reveal one. It becomes State Harm and costs two Legitimacy.
6. **Europe:** raise Europe by one if State Harm is at least three greater than PKK Harm; lower it by one if PKK Harm is at least three greater than State Harm. Then lose one Legitimacy if Europe is 4 or 5.
7. **Gains:** gain one Legitimacy for each that applies: (a) Legal Space is Open and two or more Offices are on the map; (b) Ceasefire is Reciprocal Restraint and no State Restraint Broken marker is on the map; (c) no New Displacement was flipped in step 2.
8. **Emergency choice:** the State may make one permitted move (8.2), or keep the current step. Apply any return-to-OHAL cost.

## 6.5 Legal Politics

Skip while Legal Space is Absent. This phase can first run after HEP Founded sets Legal Space to Open (5.4). In order:

1. **Closure filing.** The State may file a closure case (8.5).
2. **Procedure table.** Run the Legal Politics procedure table, lines 1 to 6 in order (8.5).
3. **Ceasefire step.** Resolve the ceasefire step (8.6).

## 6.6 State Civic Action

The State may spend in any number of eligible spaces. Each requires State Control, Troops and Police. Use 3.6.1. Total spending across all spaces cannot exceed half current Legitimacy, rounded down, and cannot exceed State Resources. The State may spend nothing.

## 6.7 PKK Agitation and Final-Round End

The PKK may spend in any number of spaces with a PKK Base or PKK Control, using 3.6.2. A Guerrilla alone without either condition is insufficient. The PKK may spend nothing.

**Final round:** after Agitation, reveal all face-down Covert markers, replacing each with State Harm and losing two Legitimacy each. Recompute final margins and determine the winner (7.3). Skip Redeploy and Reset.

## 6.8 Redeploy

In a non-final round, use Control and the piece inventory from **before** Redeploy. State moves first, then PKK. A piece moves at most once; arrivals cannot be used as new departures.

- State may move Troops to any domestic City or domestic space with a State Base. **All Troops in KDP and PUK must leave** this way.
- State may move Police to any space it Controlled before Redeploy. Guards never move. Bases never move.
- PKK may move Guerrillas to an adjacent space or box with a PKK Base. Host entry limits apply. A Restricted camp costs one Resource per destination and activates arrivals. No entry into a Hostile or Aligned camp.

Optional movements may be omitted. Required Iraqi Troop departures may not be skipped. Recompute Control after both factions finish.

## 6.9 Reset

1. Remove one Coercion from each space that has any.
2. Flip **all Guerrillas Underground**, including those in Hostile or Aligned boxes. This is an explicit Reset exception to their posture activation.
3. Discard all Momentum. Capabilities remain.
4. Remove Restraint Broken markers, if present.
5. Advance the Campaign and apply that Campaign's switches (10.8). Its structural cards still apply only when revealed. Cards left below the Propaganda card are played in the new Campaign.
6. Set PKK First Eligible, clear initiative boxes and reveal the next card.

# 7.0 Victory

## 7.1 Scores and Thresholds

**State score = Total Support + Legitimacy.**

**PKK score = Total Opposition + domestic PKK Bases.**

Passive levels contribute Pop; Active levels contribute twice Pop. Neutral contributes none. Count only domestic Support/Opposition and domestic PKK Bases. Control, external Bases and removed enemy pieces add no points.

The thresholds are **State 49** and **PKK 30**. Each faction's Harm limit is **6**. These are playtest values, not historical measures.

## 7.2 Victory Check

Check at the start of each Propaganda round except the first. A faction qualifies if its score reaches or exceeds its threshold **and** its Harm count is at most six. If only one qualifies, it wins immediately. If both qualify, compare score minus threshold. The higher margin wins. Equal margins go to lower Harm, then to PKK.

For this check only, count each face-down Covert marker as two fewer State score and one additional State Harm, without physically revealing it. This prevents an immediate victory from ignoring the hidden cost.

If neither qualifies, continue the round. Being above the Harm limit prevents victory at this check, not all possible victory at final scoring.

## 7.3 Final Scoring

After final Agitation and any required Covert reveals, calculate each faction's:

**Final margin = score − threshold − 2 × excess Harm.**

Excess Harm is the Harm count above six, or zero if the count is six or less. The higher final margin wins, even if both are negative. Equal margins go to the faction with less Harm; if still tied, PKK wins.

> **EXAMPLE:** State score 43 with Harm 4 gives 43 − 49 = −6. PKK score 26 with Harm 7 gives 26 − 30 − 2 = −6. State wins the tied margin because its Harm is lower. Without the extra PKK Harm penalty, PKK's margin would be −4.

Record a margin difference of five or less as a narrow result and six or more as a clear result. These labels describe the result; they do not change the winner.

## 7.4 Outcome Record

Record the winner, ending round, both scores, thresholds, final margins and Harm counts. Also record Legitimacy, Europe, host postures, Support/Opposition totals and any Harm or displacement remaining. Civilian effects are part of the record, not a second score or a reward.

For playtests, note repeated initiative streaks, forced Pass sequences, ambiguous rules and any harm-causing action that improves its user's relative margin. These are review flags, not extra automatic game endings.

Also record the Emergency Rule step, the Leadership side (Present, Abroad or Captured), the Ceasefire step, closure bans and successor returns, the Covert markers revealed and the first round in which a faction qualified.

# 8.0 Political and Sanctuary Systems

## 8.1 Host Postures

Syria, KDP and PUK each have a separate posture track. From most open to most closed:

**Host → Tolerant → Restricted → Hostile → Aligned with Turkey.**

Syria stops at Hostile. Postures never reset automatically and one host's change does not move another. No posture change deletes pieces.

**8.1.1 Host.** Rally may recruit Guerrillas or build Bases. March entry is normal. Each Base earns one PKK income at non-final Propaganda. Normal State Cross-Border access is closed, subject to the early KDP exception.

**8.1.2 Tolerant.** Rally may recruit Guerrillas but may not build a Base. March entry and Base income remain normal. After the Northern Iraq Vacuum card (5.4), a Tolerant KDP or PUK also allows Rally to build a PKK Base there, and allows State Cross-Border access (4.2.2).

**8.1.3 Restricted.** No Rally and no Base income. March entry costs one extra Resource and activates the group. The prototype default allows this entry into Syria-Bekaa too; the alternative option closes Syria at Restricted. Select the option before play and keep it for the session.

**8.1.4 Hostile or Aligned.** No Rally, March entry or Base income. A posture shift reaching either state activates Guerrillas in that box. Reset later flips them Underground (6.9); the exception does not reopen the box. Normal Cross-Border access to KDP/PUK is permitted. Aligned removes the extra Cross-Border cost and the Assault Mountain divisor.

**8.1.5 Leadership.** The marker starts Present in Syria-Bekaa. Present gives income only while Syria is Host or Tolerant. When Syria reaches Hostile, flip it Abroad. Abroad gives no income and prevents Host Pressure against Syria. Returning Syria to a more open posture does not by itself flip Leadership back.

**Capture.** Capture happens only through the C5 card DK-093 and only if the Leadership is Abroad. Capture is not certain: if DK-093 is revealed while the Leadership is Present, nothing happens and there is no Capture in that game. Remove the Leadership marker. From then on the PKK income is one lower at each Resources phase (never below 0). The PKK keeps its pieces and the game does not end.

## 8.2 Emergency Rule

The track is Martial Law, OHAL, OHAL Extended Powers and Normalizing. Its Propaganda drains are 1, 1, 2 and 0 respectively. OHAL Extended Powers reduces Sweep cost to one per space.

Martial Law becomes OHAL only through the OHAL card. It never returns to Martial Law. At Propaganda (6.4 step 8) the State may make one move: OHAL to Normalizing; Normalizing to OHAL at a cost of one Legitimacy; OHAL Extended Powers to OHAL; or, from C3, OHAL to OHAL Extended Powers. Every other step is free. An Event may specify its own change.

| Step | Drain | Effect |
|:---|---:|:---|
| Martial Law | 1 | None. |
| OHAL | 1 | Village Clearance is allowed (4.2.4). |
| OHAL Extended Powers | 2 | Village Clearance is allowed. Sweep costs one per space. |
| Normalizing | 0 | No Village Clearance. Return by Civic Action is allowed (3.6.1). |

Changes for single provinces are Event effects only.

## 8.3 Village Guards

All 16 start Out of Play. Law 3175 moves them to Available. Recruit them through Train or applicable Events after the Law. They are local, immobile State pieces: they count for Control and detection in their space, but not Assault. Pay upkeep at every Propaganda, including the final round unless the opening victory check already ended the game.

Guards removed by Attack, Strike Collaborators, upkeep or Events return to State Available. No Guard converts into a PKK piece. Guard Abuse is once per qualifying space (5.5).

## 8.4 Civilian Markers

**State Harm:** counts toward State Harm and blocks shifts toward Support. Redress costs three each.

**PKK Harm:** counts toward PKK Harm and blocks shifts toward Opposition. Redress costs two each.

**Disputed Harm:** counts for neither faction; three or more cost one Legitimacy at Propaganda. Ordinary Redress cannot remove it; use an applicable Event.

**Coercion:** blocks shifts both ways and counts toward PKK Harm. Remove for one Resource by Civic Action or Agitation. Reset removes one per space.

**Reception:** a receiving-city obligation. Remove for one Resource by Civic Action before shifting that space toward Support. Reception blocks every shift toward Support, from any source, until removed. Three or more cost one Legitimacy at Propaganda.

**Displacement:** blocks Rally, Extort and Coerce. It never changes Pop. New costs one Legitimacy at Propaganda, then becomes Old. Village Clearance places it (4.2.4). Return removes it under 3.6.1.

**Covert:** a face-down marker from Covert Action (4.2.5). It becomes State Harm when revealed. While face down it costs the State two points at each victory check (7.2).

**Office:** a legal-party presence in a domestic space. It gives no Control, is not a PKK piece and adds to neither victory score. It caps its space at Passive Support and Passive Opposition. Village Clearance in an Office space costs one more Legitimacy. The Legal Politics procedure places and removes Offices (8.5). Neither player chooses where.

**Closure Case and Successor:** Legal Politics markers (8.5). The Closure Case marker is on the track, not in a space. The Successor marker records the number of Offices that return after a ban.

**Europe Ban marker:** placed only by DK-064. It caps Europe at 3 for the rest of the game (1.7). It is not the Legal Politics ban (8.5 line 1).

**Restraint Broken:** a State or PKK marker placed when a faction breaks a ceasefire (8.6). Reset removes both.

## 8.5 Legal Politics

Legal Politics is a non-player system. It starts when HEP Founded sets Legal Space to Open (5.4) and runs in the Legal Politics phase (6.5). The PKK cannot move Legal Space or Offices (5.1). Legal Space has four steps: **Absent → Open → Prosecuted → Banned.**

**Closure filing.** The State may file a closure case if Legal Space is Open or Prosecuted, at least one Office is on the map and no Closure Case marker is on the track. Place the Closure Case marker and set Legal Space to Prosecuted. Filing costs nothing. An Event that places the Closure Case marker also sets Legal Space to Prosecuted. The case resolves at the next Legal Politics phase, at line 1.

**Procedure table.** Run lines 1 to 6 in order in each Legal Politics phase.

| Line | Condition | Action |
|:--:|:---|:---|
| 1 | A Closure Case marker was on the track at the start of this phase. | **Ban.** Legal Space to Banned. Set the Successor marker to the number of Offices on the map. Remove all Offices. Legitimacy −2. Europe +1. Remove the Closure Case marker. Stop. |
| 2 | Legal Space is Banned. | **Successor.** Legal Space to Prosecuted. Place Offices equal to the Successor number minus 1 (at least 1) in the line 4 order. Remove the Successor marker. Stop. |
| 3 | Legal Space is Open or Prosecuted. | **Office limit:** 4 if Open, 3 if Prosecuted. |
| 4 | Offices on the map are fewer than the limit. | Place one Office in the first eligible space in this order: Diyarbakır City, Western Cities, Mardin-Batman, Van-Bitlis, Şanlıurfa, Bingöl-Muş, Diyarbakır Rural, Botan, Ağrı, Adıyaman, Elazığ. Eligible: Pop 2 or more, no Office, no PKK Control and not at Active Support or Active Opposition. |
| 5 | Offices on the map are more than the limit. | Remove Offices until at the limit. Remove an Office outside the line 4 order first (only Hakkari or Dersim can hold one, by Event). Then remove the latest in the line 4 order. |
| 6 | Offices equal the limit and no Closure Case marker is on the track. | The prosecutor files: place the Closure Case marker and set Legal Space to Prosecuted. Line 1 bans at the next phase. |

Line 6 sees the Office count after lines 4 and 5 of the same phase. Line 2 places as many Offices as the eligible spaces allow and the rest are lost. A ban with no Offices still returns a Successor with one Office. Line 6 is a deliberate timer. No faction can stop it directly.

## 8.6 Ceasefire

The Ceasefire track has three steps: **None → Declared → Reciprocal Restraint.** A ceasefire is temporary. No negotiated settlement exists, and a ceasefire never ends the game.

**Declaration.** From C3, the PKK may Declare Ceasefire as its card action, using the Limited Op box (2.3). Ceasefire must be None. Set it to Declared and raise Europe by one. Only the PKK declares, but an Event may declare for it.

**Restrictions.**

| Ceasefire | The PKK may not | The State may not |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Declared | Attack, Ambush, Strike Collaborators | Nothing. Each State Assault, Village Clearance, Cross-Border or Covert Action places the State Restraint Broken marker. |
| Reciprocal Restraint | Attack, Ambush, Strike Collaborators, Coerce, place a domestic Base | Assault, Village Clearance, Cross-Border, Covert Action |

**Breach.** A forbidden action stays legal, but the game shows a warning before you confirm it. The acting faction places its Restraint Broken marker in the first location of the action and Ceasefire goes to None. Free actions from Events count. A PKK breach lowers Europe by one. A State breach during Reciprocal Restraint costs two Legitimacy. A State action during Declared only places the marker. The State cannot accept at that round's ceasefire step. Reset removes the marker (6.9), so the State may accept at a later round if the PKK keeps Declared.

**Ceasefire step** (in the Legal Politics phase, after the procedure table). Resolve in this order. At each sub-step the State acts first, then the PKK.

1. **Declared**, with no State Restraint Broken marker: the State may accept. Ceasefire goes to Reciprocal Restraint. If the State does not accept, the PKK keeps Declared or ends it (Ceasefire to None). If the PKK ends a ceasefire that it declared, Europe −1.
2. Either faction may leave Reciprocal Restraint (to None). The State pays no cost. The PKK loses one Europe if it leaves Reciprocal Restraint.

# 9.0 Key Terms

**Activate:** change an Underground Guerrilla to Active. It remains the same piece.

**Adjacent:** connected by an authoritative gameplay border or link, not merely near on the illustration.

**Always:** mandatory card instruction applied when revealed, before player choice.

**Available:** the supply from which pieces may be placed. Out of Play is separate.

**Base:** immobile infrastructure. Domestic PKK Bases score; external Bases do not.

**Campaign:** the current period, advanced by Reset. Some cards from the previous pile may remain after a Propaganda card.

**Capability:** a lasting Event effect that stays for the game.

**Control:** a strict piece majority in a domestic space, independent of its political level.

**First / Second Eligible:** the order of the two factions' choices on a card. Both normally receive a choice.

**Free:** ordinary Operation or SA Resource cost waived, not a waiver of every condition or political spending cost.

**Harm gate:** the maximum Harm count of six allowed for victory at an opening Propaganda check.

**Location:** a selected map space, permitted box or Europe for Rally. For movement Operations, limits usually concern destinations.

**Covert marker:** a face-down State marker from Covert Action. It is State Harm once revealed (4.2.5).


**Office:** a legal-party marker in a domestic space. It caps the space at Passive levels (8.4).

**Pivotal Event:** a card held in hand and played instead of the revealed Event (5.3).

**Margin:** score minus threshold; at final scoring, also subtract excess Harm penalties.

**Momentum:** a lasting effect discarded at Reset.

**Op / SA:** Operation / Special Activity. One SA only with a permitted Op + SA choice.

**Pop:** a fixed design weight used for political scores and some costs or placements.

**Posture:** one host's relationship and access state. No Control applies in its box.

**Redress:** pay to remove the relevant faction's Harm marker; it does not itself shift a level.

**Underground:** a Guerrilla state protected from ordinary Assault, not an absence from Control counts.

## 10.3 Build the Deck

For each Campaign separately:

1. Take its mandatory structural cards (5.4). Randomly draw from that Campaign's other eligible Event, Capability and Momentum cards until there are 12 cards in the pile. Do not include a Pivotal or the Propaganda card in these 12.
2. Shuffle the 12. Separate the top eight from the bottom four.
3. If a mandatory card is in the bottom four, exchange it with the lowest non-mandatory card in the top eight. If a chain pair ends in a mandatory card, the earlier card of that pair also receives this protection.
4. Apply chain order: if both cards of a pair below are present, put the earlier card above the later card by swapping them if necessary.
5. Shuffle the Campaign's Propaganda card into the bottom four, making five cards, and put the top eight above them. Propaganda is now in position 9–13.

## 10.5 End and Debrief

At the final Propaganda round, check victory first. If nobody wins, skip income, resolve the other steps through Agitation, reveal all Covert markers and compare final margins. Do not Redeploy or Reset after final scoring.

In the simulator, save a debrief note and export it with the session. For a physical test, write the same details on paper: seed, options, winner, final margins, Harm counts, unclear rules and any rule changes tested.
