# A.0 C1/C2 Card Reference

Current package text is reproduced below for reading beside the rules. Original card cross-references use research chapter numbers (Appendix B). U means unshaded and S shaded; either faction may choose either side unless restricted. Always instructions apply on reveal. A card need not be drawn in every game.

## A.1 Campaign C1: 1984–1986

### DK-001 · Eruh and Şemdinli (15 Aug 1984)

**U:** Martial law reinforcement: place 2 Troops from Available in Hakkari or Botan. State Resources +2.

**S:** Opening raids: place 2 Underground Guerrillas in Hakkari and 1 in Botan. Shift Botan one level toward Opposition.

### DK-002 · Raid in Siirt (17 Aug 1984)

**U:** Police alert: the State conducts a free Garrison. Its Activation must be in Botan.

**S:** Second strike: place 1 Underground Guerrilla in Botan, then the PKK conducts a free Attack in Botan with Ambush (no roll).

### DK-003 · Law 3175 (Mar 1985)

**Mandatory structural card.**

**Always:** Move all Guards from Out of Play to Available; Law 3175 is in effect.

**U:** Guard recruitment: place up to 2 Village Guards in each of up to 2 Mountain or Plain spaces with State Control and Police.

**S:** Recruitment under pressure and pay: State Resources -3. Shift one space with a Village Guard and no PKK Harm marker, or one Mountain space with State Control, one level toward Opposition.

### DK-004 · ERNK Founded (Mar 1985)

**U:** Front network exposed: remove 1 Guerrilla from Diyarbakır City or Western Cities, or PKK Resources -2 (State choice).

**S:** PKK Capability (ERNK): Agitate in a Rally may select a City with a PKK Guerrilla even without a PKK Base or PKK Control.

### DK-005 · KDP Orders PKK Out (Oct 1985)

**Mandatory structural card.**

**Always:** If KDP is Host, set its posture to Tolerant.

**U:** KDP posture one more step toward Hostile.

**S:** Guests stay: the PKK moves up to 3 Guerrillas from the KDP Zone to the PUK Zone or Hakkari, Underground, free, or places 2 Underground Guerrillas in the PUK Zone. PKK Resources +1.

### DK-006 · Chiefs Enter the Guard System (1985)

**U:** State Capability (Tribal Allies): Train may place Village Guards in a Mountain or Plain space with State Control even without Police. Needs Law 3175.

**S:** Armed retinues: apply Guard Abuse (07, section 4.7). Then shift one space with a Village Guard one level toward Opposition.

### DK-007 · ERNK Action Campaign (Jun-Nov 1985)

**U:** Police raids: the State conducts a free Garrison. Place 1 Police in Diyarbakır City.

**S:** PKK Momentum: until the next Reset, each Rally may Agitate in one extra space (normal cost).

### DK-008 · Former Member Killed Abroad (Nov 1985)

**U:** Europe -1. PKK Resources -1.

**S:** Damage control: Europe -1. PKK Resources +1.

### DK-009 · Bekaa Training Academy (1986)

**U:** Camp watched: the State conducts a free Diplomacy targeting Syria with +1.

**S:** PKK Capability (Academy): while Syria is Host or Tolerant, a Rally in Syria-Bekaa places up to 2 extra Guerrillas there.

### DK-010 · Rival Groups Form the TSK (1986)

**U:** Recruits divided: PKK Resources -2. State Momentum: until the next Reset, a PKK Rally in a Mountain space places 1 fewer Guerrilla (minimum 1).

**S:** Rivals do not field a force: PKK Resources +2. Place 1 Underground Guerrilla in Van-Bitlis or Ağrı.

### DK-011 · PKK Spring Offensive (Mar 1986)

**U:** Patrol security: the State conducts a free Sweep in up to 2 Mountain spaces.

**S:** PKK Momentum: until the next Reset, Ambush may select up to 3 Attack spaces.

### DK-012 · Ankara Names a Guerrilla War (May 1985)

**U:** War budget: State Resources +4. Place 2 Troops from Available in any Mountain spaces.

**S:** Recognition of the conflict: Europe +1. PKK Resources +1.

### DK-013 · Border Attack and Air Raid into Northern Iraq (Aug 1986)

**U:** Hot pursuit raid: the State conducts a free Assault with Cross-Border in the KDP Zone (06, section 3.2, hot pursuit: at most 3 Troops). No extra Resources.

**S:** Camps rebuilt after the raid: the PKK places up to 2 Underground Guerrillas in the KDP Zone if its posture is Host or Tolerant. PKK Resources +1.

### DK-014 · Human Rights Association Founded (Jul 1986)

**U:** Tolerated civil society: Legitimacy +1.

**S:** Documentation begins: Europe +1.

### DK-016 · Propaganda

**Propaganda:** conduct 6.0. No player Event choice.

### DK-100 · Neighbors Condemn the Raid (Sep 1986)

**U:** Ankara names the raid a reprisal for the loss of its soldiers: Legitimacy +1. State Resources +1.

**S:** Iran and Libya condemn the raid: shift Syria posture one step toward Host, or Europe +1 if Syria is already Host. PKK Resources +1.

## A.2 Campaign C2: 1987–1989

### DK-017 · Air Raids on Camps in Iraq (Mar 1987)

**U:** Remove 1 Guerrilla from the KDP Zone, or 1 PKK Base there if no Guerrilla remains. Place a Disputed Harm marker in the KDP Zone if it has none.

**S:** Camps disperse: move up to 3 Guerrillas from the KDP Zone to the PUK Zone or Hakkari, Underground.

### DK-018 · Killings at Pınarcık (Jun 1987)

**U:** Place a Disputed Harm marker in Mardin-Batman. Place up to 2 Village Guards there (needs Law 3175). Europe -1.

**S:** Place a Disputed Harm marker in Mardin-Batman. The PKK may pay 2 Resources to remove 1 PKK Harm marker anywhere.

### DK-019 · Syria Security Protocol (Jul 1987)

**Mandatory structural card.**

**Always:** If Syria is Host, set its posture to Tolerant.

**U:** The State conducts a free Diplomacy targeting Syria with +1.

**S:** Paper limits: Syria posture one step toward Host. The PKK conducts a free Rally in Syria-Bekaa.

### DK-020 · OHAL (Jul 1987)

**Mandatory structural card.**

**Always:** If Emergency Rule is Martial Law, set it to OHAL.

**U:** Regional governor: the State conducts a free Garrison, then a free Sweep in one space.

**S:** Emergency rule draws scrutiny: Legitimacy -1. Europe +1.

### DK-021 · Attacks on Rival Newroz Gatherings (Mar 1987)

**U:** Place a Disputed Harm marker in Botan or Mardin-Batman (State choice). Europe -1.

**S:** Block only: place a Disputed Harm marker in Botan or Mardin-Batman (PKK choice). No other effect.

### DK-022 · Special Strike Force Planned (Jul 1987)

**U:** State Capability (Strike Force): Air Mobility may move up to 6 Troops among up to 4 spaces.

**S:** Plan stalls: State Resources -3. PKK Resources +1.

### DK-023 · Final Anfal in Badinan (Aug 1988)

**U:** Border sealed: place 2 Troops in Hakkari and 1 in Botan.

**S:** World attention to Kurds: Europe +1.

### DK-024 · Iraqi Kurdish Refugees Admitted (Aug-Sep 1988)

**U:** Humanitarian opening: Legitimacy +2. State Resources -2.

**S:** Camps under strain: place 1 Reception marker in Diyarbakır City. Europe +1.

### DK-025 · Attacks on Village Schools (Nov 1988)

**U:** Place a PKK Harm marker in Mardin-Batman, Elazığ or Dersim (State choice). Europe -1.

**S:** Block only: place a PKK Harm marker in one of those spaces (PKK choice). No other effect.

### DK-026 · Local Elections (Mar 1989)

**U:** Institutions renewed: Legitimacy +2.

**S:** Opposition mayors in the southeast: shift Diyarbakır City or Mardin-Batman one level toward Opposition.

### DK-027 · Regional Force Doubled (1989)

**U:** Place up to 4 Troops from Available in Botan and Hakkari. The State conducts a free Sweep in Botan.

**S:** Rising tempo: the PKK conducts a free Attack in one space with an Underground Guerrilla and Troops. State Resources -2.

### DK-028 · Paris Kurdish Conference (Oct 1989)

**U:** Diplomatic protest: the State conducts a free Diplomacy targeting the Europe track.

**S:** Europe +1. The PKK conducts a free Rally on the Europe track.

### DK-029 · SHP Expels Kurdish Deputies (Nov 1989)

**U:** Party discipline holds: shift Şanlıurfa or Adıyaman one level toward Support if it has State Control and Police.

**S:** Expelled deputies speak abroad: Europe +1. Shift Diyarbakır City one level toward Opposition.

### DK-030 · İkikaya Village Killings (Nov 1989)

**U:** Place a PKK Harm marker in Hakkari. Europe -1. Place 1 Village Guard in Hakkari (needs Law 3175).

**S:** Block only: place a PKK Harm marker in Hakkari. No other effect.

### DK-031 · Çukurca Villagers Refuse Guard Duty (1989)

**U:** Prosecutions hold the line: place 1 Police and 1 Village Guard in Hakkari.

**S:** Guards hand back weapons: remove up to 2 Village Guards from Hakkari or Botan to Available. No Harm marker. State Resources -1.

### DK-032 · Lawyer Access Decree (Sep 1989)

**U:** Custody reform: Legitimacy +2.

**S:** Lawyers barred again: Legitimacy -1. Europe +1.

### DK-033 · Abuse and Killings in Custody Reported (1989)

**U:** Block only: place a State Harm marker in Mardin-Batman or Hakkari (State choice). No other effect.

**S:** Place a State Harm marker in Mardin-Batman or Hakkari (PKK choice). Legitimacy -1. Shift that space one level toward Opposition.

### DK-034 · Governors Exile Local Party Members (Sep 1989)

**U:** Emergency powers: the State conducts a free Garrison with Activation in Botan or Dersim.

**S:** Banishment without trial: Legitimacy -1. Shift Botan one level toward Opposition.

### DK-035 · Propaganda

**Propaganda:** conduct 6.0. No player Event choice.

## A.3 PKK Pivotal in Hand

### DK-015 · ARGK Formed

At least eight domestic Guerrillas and two domestic PKK Bases. Place up to four Available Guerrillas at PKK Bases, flip all Guerrillas in every space with a PKK Base Underground and gain three PKK Resources. DEC-55 clarifies the original card's phrase ‘in those spaces’. See 5.3 for timing and initiative.
