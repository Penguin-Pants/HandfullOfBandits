# 06 Operations and Special Activities

Status: rules draft. All costs and limits are design values. Values marked [tune] need playtest calibration. CT means Colonial Twilight rules. Each action ends with an evidence line: research IDs, then the CT rule it adapts. Every mechanic has status Hypothesis (DD-15.1).

## 1. General rules

1. An Operation (Op) selects one or more locations and pays its cost per location, unless the text says "total".
2. A Special Activity (SA) needs an Op + SA choice. It accompanies only the Ops listed for it. One SA per action. Never with a Limited Op (CT 4.1).
3. An SA may happen before, during or after its Op, unless the text says otherwise. It may select spaces that the Op did not select, unless the text says otherwise.
4. A faction that cannot pay the full cost of a location cannot select it (CT 1.8).
5. Detection before destruction: Underground Guerrillas are never removed by Assault, Air Mobility or Cross-Border Assault. They must be Activated first by Sweep, Garrison, Village Clearance, PKK actions or events (CT 1.4.3, 3.2.4). Covert Action is the one exception and carries a hidden cost.
6. Mountain modifier: in Mountain spaces, KDP Zone and PUK Zone, activation is one Guerrilla per 2 eligible State pieces and Assault is one removal per 2 Troops, round down (CT 3.2.2 to 3.2.4).
7. No action gives victory points for removing pieces. Removing a PKK Base lowers the PKK score because the Base no longer exists. It never adds to the State score (DD-07.4; M-028).
8. Ceasefire restrictions are in 07, section 7. An action that a ceasefire forbids may still be taken. It breaks the ceasefire with the costs in 07.

## 2. State Operations

### 2.1 Train (recruit and train)

Cost: 2 Resources per selected space.

Select any of:

1. A City (Diyarbakır City, Western Cities) or a space with a State Base: place up to 3 Troops and Police, in any mix, from Available.
2. After the Law 3175 event: a Mountain or Plain space with State Control and at least 1 Police: place up to 2 Village Guards from Available. Maximum 3 Guards in a space.
3. A Mountain or Plain space with State Control, at least 2 Police and fewer than 2 State Bases: replace 2 Police with 1 State Base.

A space may use only one of the three options per Train.

Civic Action: in one selected space, even in a Limited Op, the State may pay for Civic Action as in 07, section 1.3. The space needs State Control and Police. Civic Action costs Resources even when Train is free (CT 3.2.1).

Evidence: Guards only after Law 3175, adopted 26 Mar 1985 and published 4 Apr 1985 [E-0004; E-0005; F-010]. Recruitment under pressure and pay [F-011; Q-347]. Supervision by gendarmerie, shown by the Police requirement [O-092; O-010]. Adapts CT 3.2.1 (Train, Pacify).

### 2.2 Garrison

Cost: 2 Resources total.

1. Move up to 6 Police from any domestic spaces to any domestic spaces, except into or out of Western Cities.
2. Then select one space with Police. Activate one Underground Guerrilla there per Police plus Village Guard in that space. In a Mountain space, one per 2, round down.
3. Limited Op: all moved Police go to one destination. Activation happens there.

Evidence: area presence and local knowledge; Guards add to detection, not to Assault [F-012; F-034; F-049]. Adapts CT 3.2.2.

### 2.3 Sweep

Cost: 2 Resources per selected space. At OHAL Extended Powers: 1 Resource per space [tune].

1. Select domestic spaces other than Western Cities. (KDP Zone and PUK Zone only through Cross-Border, section 3.2.)
2. Move any Troops from adjacent spaces into each selected space. Troops move one step only. Police and Guards do not move.
3. In each selected space, Activate one Underground Guerrilla per Troop, Police and Guard there. In a Mountain space, one per 2, round down.
4. Limited Op: one selected space; Troops may come from several adjacent spaces.

Evidence: area domination and large deployments [E-0202; E-0204; Q-341; F-036]. Emergency governor powers reduce friction [E-0058; F-013]. Adapts CT 3.2.3.

### 2.4 Assault

Cost: 2 Resources per selected space.

1. Select spaces with Troops and Active PKK pieces. (KDP Zone and PUK Zone only through Cross-Border.)
2. Remove one PKK piece per Troop. In a Mountain space, one per 2 Troops, round down. In a City, Police count as Troops.
3. Remove Active Guerrillas first. Remove PKK Bases only when no Guerrillas, Underground or Active, remain in the space. Never remove Underground Guerrillas.
4. Removed pieces go to PKK Available.

Evidence: State military advantage limited by concealment; camps rebuilt after operations [F-049; F-059]. Adapts CT 3.2.4. CT gives +1 Commitment per Base removed; this design gives nothing (DD-07.4).

## 3. State Special Activities

### 3.1 Air Mobility

Accompanies: any Op.

Move up to 4 Troops among up to 3 domestic spaces, including Western Cities. The 3 spaces include origins and destinations (CT 4.2.2 pattern). Never into an external box.

Evidence: helicopter and commando mobility in large operations [O-063; F-059]. Adapts CT 4.2.2 (Troop Lift).

### 3.2 Cross-Border

Accompanies: Sweep or Assault.

Access:

1. KDP Zone: legal if KDP posture is Restricted, Hostile or Aligned with Turkey.
2. PUK Zone: legal if PUK posture is Restricted, Hostile or Aligned with Turkey and KDP posture is also one of these.
3. Hot pursuit, C1 and C2 only: the KDP Zone is legal at any KDP posture, with at most 3 Troops [E-0154; Q-380; F-024 is Low].
4. From C3, after the Northern Iraq Vacuum card: also legal at Tolerant [F-023; E-0176].
5. Never Syria-Bekaa or Iran.

Procedure:

1. Pay 2 Resources in addition to the Op cost. Pay 0 extra if the target host is Aligned with Turkey [tune].
2. Move up to 6 Troops from Hakkari or Botan into the zone. For the PUK Zone, Troops may pass through the KDP Zone in this move.
3. Conduct the Sweep or Assault in the zone as one selected location. The zone is Mountain terrain. If the host is Aligned with Turkey, ignore the Mountain modifier for Assault (local allied forces) [E-0082].
4. If the Assault removes at least one PKK piece and the zone has no Disputed Harm marker, place one. Civilian harm in these operations is not verified in the research. The marker records "unknown", not "none".
5. State Troops stay in the zone until the next Redeploy phase. They give no Control.

Evidence: operations in Aug 1991, Oct 1992, Mar to May 1995, May to Jul 1997 and Sep to Oct 1997 [E-0077; E-0083; E-0108; E-0120; F-059; Q-342]. Adapts CT 1.3.4 and 5.1.8 (no entry to Countries) with a restricted-entry exception.

### 3.3 Diplomacy

Accompanies: any Op.

1. Pay 2 Resources.
2. Choose one target: Syria posture, KDP posture, PUK posture or the Europe track.
3. Roll one die. Modifiers: +1 if Legitimacy is 12 or more; -1 if Legitimacy is 5 or less; -1 for Syria before C5; +1 for KDP from C3.
4. Modified 5 or more: shift the posture one step toward Hostile, or lower Europe by 1. A shift from Hostile to Aligned with Turkey needs a modified 6 or more.
5. Otherwise no effect. Diplomacy never moves two hosts.

Evidence: different instruments per host [F-056]. Syria yielded to force, KDP turned for revenue and alignment [F-051; F-020; F-022]. No automatic reset (DD-09.4). Original design; posture tracks adapt ADP 6.6.1 without its reset.

### 3.4 Village Clearance

Accompanies: Sweep or Garrison.

Conditions, all required:

1. Emergency Rule is OHAL or OHAL Extended Powers.
2. The OHAL Powers event has been played, or it is C3 or later (DD-07.2).
3. The target is one Mountain or Plain space selected for the Op. It has State Control, Troops and Police. It is Neutral or Opposition. It has no Displacement marker.
4. Legitimacy is at least (2 x target Pop) + 1.

Effects, in order:

1. Shift the space one level toward Neutral (DD-18.3).
2. Legitimacy -2 x Pop of the space, at once.
3. Place a Displacement marker, New side up. It costs Legitimacy 1 more at the next Propaganda round (05, section 6.4).
4. Place a State Harm marker.
5. Place a Reception marker in Diyarbakır City or Western Cities, State choice. If none is available, Legitimacy -1 instead.
6. Activate all Guerrillas in the space.
7. If the space holds an Office, Legitimacy -1 more.

The space keeps its Pop for victory (DD-07.3). While the Displacement marker stays, the PKK cannot Rally, Extort or Coerce there.

Evidence: mass evacuation in 1992-95 as a main tool, with no written order found [F-031 is Low; E-0200; Q-265; E-0204]. Contested totals [F-032; Q-273; Q-279]. Early logistics gain, later cost [F-050; M-005]. Adapts CT 4.2.1 (Resettle) but rejects its Pop 0 rule (CT lessons).

### 3.5 Covert Action

Accompanies: Garrison or Sweep. Available from C3. Needs Legitimacy 4 or more.

1. Select one space with State Control, Police and at least one Underground Guerrilla.
2. Remove one Underground Guerrilla. No activation is needed.
3. Place one face-down Covert marker in the space. If no Covert marker is available, Covert Action is not allowed.
4. Covert Action never targets an Office or any non-player marker.

A Covert marker is revealed by events, by the exposure step (05, section 6.4) or before final scoring. When revealed, it becomes a State Harm marker and Legitimacy -2.

Evidence: covert networks found in part by courts and commissions [F-028; E-0015]; unidentified murders 1991 to 1994 with no attribution in the data [F-029]; late reports [F-030]; M-009. Evidence level: alleged; print it on the aid. Original design; no CT analogue.

## 4. PKK Operations

### 4.1 Rally

Cost: 1 Resource per selected location.

Locations:

1. Any domestic space without a Displacement marker. A City only if it is Neutral or Opposition, except that Western Cities may also be selected at Passive Support or Active Support (03, section 2.1) [tune].
2. An external box whose host posture permits (07, section 5).
3. The Europe track: Europe +1. With the Ban marker, the cost is 2 and Europe cannot rise above 3.

In each selected space or box:

1. Place 1 Guerrilla from Available, or
2. Replace 2 Guerrillas with 1 PKK Base (stacking limits apply; never in Western Cities; external Bases only where the host is Host, or Tolerant in the KDP Zone and PUK Zone after the Northern Iraq Vacuum card (07, section 5.1)), or
3. If a PKK Base is there: place Guerrillas up to Pop + PKK Bases there (an external box counts Pop 1), or flip all Guerrillas there Underground.

Agitate: in one selected space with a PKK Base or PKK Control, even in a Limited Op, the PKK may pay for Agitation as in 07, section 1.3. Agitate costs Resources even when Rally is free.

Evidence: sanctuary and recruitment [F-007; F-051]; Europe as diaspora source [F-040; M-013]; Offices cap Agitation at Passive Opposition (DD-18.4). Adapts CT 3.3.1, with the Europe track in the role of the France Track.

### 4.2 March

Cost: 1 Resource per destination.

1. Move any Guerrillas from adjacent spaces or boxes into each destination. One step only.
2. Entry into an external box: Host or Tolerant, normal; Restricted, pay 1 more Resource and the group Activates; Hostile or Aligned with Turkey, not allowed. Leaving a box is always allowed.
3. No March into or out of Western Cities.
4. Activation: a moving group flips Active if (the destination is at Support, or the group crosses into or out of an external box) and (group size + State pieces in the destination) is more than 3.
5. Limited Op: one destination.

Evidence: movement between sanctuary and mountain spaces [F-007; F-023]; host limits [F-022]. Adapts CT 3.3.2.

### 4.3 Attack

Cost: 1 Resource per selected space.

1. Select spaces with Guerrillas and State pieces.
2. Activate all Guerrillas there.
3. Roll one die. If the result is equal to or less than the number of PKK Guerrillas in the space, remove up to 2 State pieces: Troops or Police first (PKK choice), then Village Guards, then State Bases.
4. Attrition: for each Troop removed, remove one Guerrilla from the space.
5. On a roll of 1, also place 1 Underground Guerrilla from Available in the space.
6. Removed State pieces go to State Available.

Evidence: attacks on army patrols and stations [E-0003; E-0073; F-009]. Adapts CT 3.3.3.

### 4.4 Coerce

Cost: 1 Resource per selected space, 2 in a space with Pop 3 or more [tune] (DD-19.4).

1. Select spaces with an Underground Guerrilla that are Neutral or Support and have no Displacement marker.
2. Activate one Guerrilla in each.
3. If the space has no Coercion marker, place one. If it was at Support, shift it one level toward Neutral.
4. Coerce never shifts a space toward Opposition (DD-06.5).

Coercion markers count toward the PKK Harm count (04, section 4.2).

Evidence: both armed sides pressed the same villages; compliance is not support [F-034; F-012]. Adapts CT 3.3.4 (Terror), with a one-level shift in place of "set to Neutral" to fit the five-level scale.

## 5. PKK Special Activities

### 5.1 Ambush

Accompanies: Attack.

In up to 2 Attack spaces that have an Underground Guerrilla: do not roll. Activate only one Underground Guerrilla. Remove 1 State piece in the Attack order. No attrition.

Evidence: guerrilla tactics against patrols [E-0162]. Adapts CT 4.3.3.

### 5.2 Extort

Accompanies: any Op.

In each space with Pop 1 or more, an Underground Guerrilla, PKK Control and no Displacement marker, and in each external box whose host is Host or Tolerant and that holds an Underground Guerrilla: Activate one Guerrilla and gain 1 Resource.

Evidence: taxation mixed willing and coerced payment; Low confidence [F-034; Q-359 not used for values]. Adapts CT 4.3.1.

### 5.3 Strike Collaborators

Accompanies: Rally, March or Coerce. Needs the Law 3175 event.

1. Select up to 2 spaces with an Underground Guerrilla and at least one Village Guard. Pay 1 Resource per space.
2. Remove up to 2 Guards in total to State Available.
3. Activate one Guerrilla in each selected space.
4. Place a PKK Harm marker in each selected space.
5. Europe -1, once per action.

Strike Collaborators never shifts Support or Opposition. Its gain is military only. A removed Guard never becomes a PKK piece (DD-08.4).

Evidence: guards were the PKK's main local target; attacks also hit families and teachers [F-012; F-034; E-0169]. Cost in European space [F-040]. Rejects CT 4.3.2 (Subvert) per CT lessons.

### 5.4 Host Pressure

Accompanies: Rally or March.

1. Select one host (Syria, KDP or PUK) whose box holds a PKK piece. Not Syria while the Leadership is Abroad.
2. Pay 2 Resources. Roll one die.
3. Syria or PUK: 4 to 6, shift one step toward Host; 1, shift one step toward Hostile (backfire); 2 to 3, no effect.
4. KDP: 5 to 6, shift one step toward Host; 1 to 2, shift one step toward Hostile (backfire); 3 to 4, no effect.
5. If Host Pressure before Rally or March makes planned locations illegal, retain the roll and paid SA cost. The PKK chooses new legal locations for the same Operation before resolution continues (DEC-54; DEC-56).

Evidence: the 1992 attempt to force host tolerance turned the KDP and PUK against the PKK [F-041 is Low; E-0080; E-0082]; host interests governed sanctuary [F-051]. Original design using the ADP 6.6.1 posture idea.

Europe mobilization is not a separate SA. The PKK raises Europe by selecting the Europe track as a Rally location (section 4.1). This keeps one route, as CT does with the France Track (CT 3.3.1).

## 7. Worked example

The State has 10 Resources. Botan (Mountain) holds 4 Troops, 1 Police, 2 Guards and 2 Underground Guerrillas. The State chooses Op + SA: Sweep in Botan (2 Resources). It Activates (4 + 1 + 2) / 2 = 3, round down, so both Guerrillas flip Active. As its SA it takes Air Mobility and moves 2 more Troops into Botan. It cannot Assault on the same action, because Sweep was its Op. Next card the PKK is First Eligible (CT 2.3.5). The PKK may March the Active Guerrillas out, or Rally to flip them Underground if a PKK Base is there. Coverage and tempo compete, as in CT lessons example 1.

## 8. Tests

1. Atrocity test (Claude harm file, section 6): for Village Clearance, Covert Action and Strike Collaborators, compute the net score change for the user at the next victory check. 09, section 7 gives the arithmetic. Any of these with a positive net score effect fails. Coerce is intimidation, not killing. It may shift a score (DD-06.5; CT 3.3.4) but its markers count toward the PKK Harm gate. 09, section 7 checks that bound.
2. Check that Cross-Border cannot end the game by itself. Count PKK Bases rebuilt in the KDP Zone within one Campaign.
3. Check Diplomacy odds. If a posture moves more than twice per Campaign by Diplomacy, raise the target to 6.
