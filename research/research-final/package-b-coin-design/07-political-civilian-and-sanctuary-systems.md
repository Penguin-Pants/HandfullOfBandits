# 07 Political, Civilian and Sanctuary Systems

Status: rules draft. Binding decisions: DD-06 to DD-12. All numbers are design values. Values marked [tune] need playtest calibration. Every mechanic has status Hypothesis (DD-15.1).

## 1. Support and Opposition

### 1.1 Principle

Control, Support and Legitimacy are separate (DD-06; M-004). Control comes from pieces. Support and Opposition are per space. Legitimacy is national. No operation turns Control into Support. Support and Opposition abstract active minorities and mixed attitudes. They do not measure ethnic preference (CT playbook pp.13-14; F-034; F-049).

### 1.2 Levels and shifts

Each populated domestic space is at Active Support, Passive Support, Neutral, Passive Opposition or Active Opposition (04, section 4.1). A shift moves one level. Blocks:

1. A Coercion marker blocks all shifts in its space until it is removed.
2. A State Harm marker blocks shifts toward Support in its space.
3. A PKK Harm marker blocks shifts toward Opposition in its space.
4. An Office caps its space at Passive Support and at Passive Opposition (DD-18.4). The cap stops shifts beyond these levels. The procedure table never places an Office in a space at an Active level (section 6.4, line 4). The legal party is an independent alternative that neither armed side owns (DD-10.5).
5. Coerce and Strike Collaborators never shift a space toward Opposition (DD-06.5).

### 1.3 Civic Action and Agitation procedures

Civic Action (State). Used in Train (one space) and in the Propaganda phase (any number of spaces, total spending at most half of Legitimacy, round down [tune]; halved after the desk test in 11, IR-02). The space needs State Control and at least one Police (DD-06.4). In the Propaganda phase it also needs at least one Troop, as CT 6.3.1 requires Troops and Police [tune]. Reason: fidelity to CT 6.3.1. It does not by itself fix the State scoring pace found in the Stage 0 desk test (11, section 1). Costs, in any order the State chooses, except that a shift needs the blocks removed first:

| Step | Cost |
|---|---|
| Remove one Coercion marker | 1 |
| Relief: remove one Reception marker | 1 |
| Redress: remove one State Harm marker | 3 |
| Return: remove one Displacement marker (section 3.3) | 3 |
| Shift one level toward Support (maximum 2 shifts per space per phase or action) | 2 |

Agitation (PKK). Used in Rally (one space with a PKK Base or PKK Control) and in the Propaganda phase (spaces with a PKK piece and no State Control). In a space with an Office, Agitation may not shift the space beyond Passive Opposition (DD-18.4).

| Step | Cost |
|---|---|
| Remove one Coercion marker | 1 |
| Redress: remove one PKK Harm marker | 2 |
| Shift one level toward Opposition (maximum 2 shifts per space per phase or action) | 1 |

Evidence: pacification needs capacity and spending, and spending must not buy consent by itself (CT lessons table). Adapts CT 3.2.1, 3.3.1, 6.3.1 and 6.3.2. The cost gap (State 2, PKK 1) follows CT.

## 3. Harm and displacement (DD-07)

### 3.1 Harm markers

1. A Harm marker records civilian harm in a space. Its side names the State, the PKK or "Disputed" (DD-07.1).
2. Sources: Village Clearance (State), revealed Covert marker (State), Strike Collaborators (PKK), Cross-Border Assault (Disputed, zone only), events (as printed; Disputed when attribution is contested).
3. A Harm marker stays until Redress or an event removes it. It never leaves by itself.
4. Equal costs do not claim equal acts (DD-15.3). Card text prints the evidence level.
5. Harm markers in external boxes count for the Harm count but do not block shifts (boxes have no Support).

### 3.2 Displacement and Reception

1. Village Clearance places one Displacement marker (New side) in the cleared space and one Reception marker in Diyarbakır City or Western Cities (06, section 3.4).
2. Displacement never lowers Pop for victory (DD-07.3). The CT Resettle Pop 0 rule is rejected (CT 4.2.1; CT lessons).
3. While a Displacement marker is present: the PKK cannot Rally, Extort or Coerce in the space. That is the State's gain: cut logistics [F-050; F-031].
4. The displaced space stays an obligation. It keeps the level that Village Clearance left (one level toward Neutral, DD-18.3) and scores at that level.
5. A Reception marker is a receiving-city obligation. The State must pay relief (1 each) before it can shift that space toward Support. Reception never places a PKK piece or an Office (Sol political systems file; M-018).
6. No victory condition counts displacement as a positive (DD-07.4).

### 3.3 Return

Return removes one Displacement marker for 3 Resources by Civic Action. It is legal only in C5, at Normalizing, or by a Return event [E-0136]. It never removes the Harm marker in the space and gives no Support. Return was small in history: 26,481 returnees by end 1999 per government figures [Q-281]. Displacement totals stay contested [F-032].

## 4. Village Guards (DD-08)

1. Guards enter play only after the Law 3175 event [E-0004; E-0005; F-010].
2. Placement: Train in a Mountain or Plain space with State Control and Police (06, section 2.1).
3. A Guard stays in its home space. It counts for Control there. It never moves by Sweep, Air Mobility, Garrison or Redeploy (DD-08.2).
4. Guards add to Sweep and Garrison activation in their space (local knowledge). They do not add to Assault.
5. Upkeep: 1 Resource per 2 Guards each Propaganda round (DD-08.3) [Q-347].
6. Removal: PKK Attack (after Troops and Police), Strike Collaborators (with a PKK Harm marker), upkeep failure, events. A removed Guard never becomes a PKK piece (DD-08.4).
7. Guard Abuse events: when such a card is executed, in every space where a Guard shares a space with any Harm marker, Legitimacy -1 and remove one Guard there, once per qualifying space (DEC-57). Card text says "some guards", not a share [F-012; Q-193 is Low].
8. Guards have no victory score and no independent aim [F-057].

### 5.1 Posture effects

Syria-Bekaa never allows Cross-Border. The C1 and C2 hot pursuit exception for the KDP Zone is in 06, section 3.2.

### 5.2 Rules

1. Each posture moves on its own. Closing one never moves another (DD-09.1).
2. Moves: State Diplomacy (06, section 3.3), PKK Host Pressure (06, section 5.4), events, State Pivotal "Coercive Diplomacy on Syria". No automatic reset (DD-09.4; ADP 6.6.1 reset rejected).
3. A posture change never removes pieces. It suspends Rally and income (DD-09.5).
4. Leadership: the marker sits in Syria-Bekaa on its Present side. When Syria reaches Hostile, flip it to Abroad. While Abroad it gives no income and Host Pressure on Syria is not allowed. Capture is an event effect only, on the mandatory C5 Capture card, and applies only if the Leadership is Abroad (DD-09.6) [E-0018; E-0130; E-0020; F-021].
5. After Capture: remove the Leadership marker. PKK income -1 per Propaganda for the rest of the game [tune]. The PKK keeps its pieces. Capture does not end the game (CT lessons; F-021; F-045).

Evidence: Syria phases [F-020; E-0170; E-0078; E-0062]; KDP order of 1985, Oct 1992 attack, later alignment [E-0160; E-0082; F-022]; PUK as the more tolerant host [F-022]; host interests [F-051]. Iran stays event-only [F-025; E-0166].

## 6. Legal Politics (DD-10)

### 6.1 Scope

Legal Politics is a non-player system. It switches on in C3 with the HEP Founded card [E-0007; O-030]. In C1 and C2 Legal Space is Absent and this section does nothing. The rule set revises the Claude two-faction table (Claude scenarios file, section 3.4).

### 6.2 What Offices do

1. Offices sit in domestic spaces. They give no Control and are not PKK pieces (DD-10.2).
2. Agitation in their space may not go beyond Passive Opposition (DD-18.4).
3. They cap their space at Passive Support and at Passive Opposition (DD-18.4).
4. They add to neither victory score (DD-10.5).
5. They change Legitimacy: +1 at Propaganda if Legal Space is Open and 2 or more Offices are on the map; -1 extra for Village Clearance in an Office space; -2 for a ban.
6. The PKK cannot move Legal Space or Offices (DD-10.5). No PKK action reads or changes them. The game does not decide whether the parties acted independently of the PKK [F-018].

### 6.3 Closure filing

In the Legal Politics phase, before the procedure table, the State may file a closure case if Legal Space is Open or Prosecuted, at least one Office is on the map and no Closure Case marker is on the track. Place the Closure Case marker. Set Legal Space to Prosecuted. The case resolves at the next Propaganda round, line 1 below. Filing itself costs nothing (DD-10.4 puts the cost on the ban). An event that places the Closure Case marker also sets Legal Space to Prosecuted, as a filing does. Only the State executes such an event side (05, section 5.1).

The State's reason to file: Offices cap Diyarbakır City and Western Cities at Passive Support (and at Passive Opposition). The cost: Legitimacy -2 and Europe +1 at the ban, and loss of the Open-Space Legitimacy gain.

### 6.4 Procedure table

Run lines 1 to 6 in order in each Legal Politics phase from C3.

| Line | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Closure Case marker was on the track at the start of this phase | Ban: Legal Space to Banned. Place the Successor marker at the number of Offices on the map. Remove all Offices. Legitimacy -2. Europe +1. Remove the Closure Case marker. Stop. |
| 2 | Legal Space is Banned (line 1 did not apply) | Successor: Legal Space to Prosecuted. Place Offices equal to the Successor number minus 1 (minimum 1), using the line 4 order. Remove the Successor marker. Stop. |
| 3 | Legal Space is Open or Prosecuted | Office limit: Open 4, Prosecuted 3 [tune]. |
| 4 | Offices on the map are fewer than the limit | Place 1 Office in the first eligible space in this order: Diyarbakır City, Western Cities, Mardin-Batman, Van-Bitlis, Şanlıurfa, Bingöl-Muş, Diyarbakır Rural, Botan, Ağrı, Adıyaman, Elazığ. Eligible: Pop 2 or more, no Office, no PKK Control, not at Active Support or Active Opposition. |
| 5 | Offices on the map are more than the limit | Remove Offices from the eligible spaces latest in the line 4 order until at the limit. |
| 6 | Offices on the map equal the limit and no Closure Case marker is on the track | The prosecutor files: place the Closure Case marker. Line 1 bans at the next phase (DD-19.1). |

Notes:

1. Line 4 is a design convenience. It stops either armed player from choosing where legal politics exists (Sol non-player actors file).
2. Line 2 makes the successor return at the next round with fewer Offices (DD-10.3). History had successors ready before the ban: HADEP was founded 36 days before the DEP ban [E-0013; E-0014; F-015; F-052].
3. Events may place or remove Offices and move Legal Space as printed: HEP closure [E-0186; E-0009], DEP founding and closure [E-0010; E-0197; E-0014], HADEP founding and filing [E-0013; E-0236].
4. The 10 percent threshold kept the legal parties out of parliament [E-0153; F-016]. Offices stand for local presence, not seats.
5. Line 6 is the non-player prosecutor. Closure cases were brought by the Chief Public Prosecutor at the Court of Cassation [O-116], not by the cabinet. The State may still file earlier by choice (section 6.3). Line 6 keeps the founding, ban and successor cycle alive when the State player has no reason to file (IR-33; F-052).

## 7. Ceasefire and talks (DD-11)

### 7.1 Declaration

From C3, the PKK may Declare Ceasefire as its action on a card, using the Limited Op box (05, section 2.3). Ceasefire must be None. Set it to Declared. Europe +1. Events may also declare for the PKK. Only the PKK declares (DD-11.2) [E-0089; E-0112; E-0123; E-0135; F-039].

### 7.2 Restrictions and breaches

| Ceasefire | PKK may not | State may not |
|---|---|---|
| Declared | Attack, Ambush, Strike Collaborators | No restriction. Each State Assault, Village Clearance, Cross-Border or Covert Action places the State Restraint Broken marker |
| Reciprocal Restraint | Attack, Ambush, Strike Collaborators, Coerce, place a domestic Base (DD-19.3) | Assault, Village Clearance, Cross-Border, Covert Action |
| Talks | As Reciprocal Restraint | As Reciprocal Restraint |

Breach: a faction that takes a forbidden action places its Restraint Broken marker and the Ceasefire goes to None. Free actions granted by events count as actions for this table. PKK breach: Europe -1. State breach (Reciprocal Restraint or Talks only): Legitimacy -2. A spoiler event may set the Ceasefire to None with no Restraint Broken marker and a neutral title; attribution stays as the sources state it [E-0091; E-0092; E-0093; F-038; F-053].

### 7.3 Ceasefire step (Propaganda, Legal Politics phase)

1. Declared, no State Restraint Broken marker: the State may accept. Ceasefire to Reciprocal Restraint. If it does not, the PKK keeps Declared or ends it (None). If the PKK ends a ceasefire that it declared by section 7.1, Europe -1, so a declaration that is not kept returns its Europe gain [tune].
2. Reciprocal Restraint that was already in force at the previous Propaganda round, with no breach since: each faction secretly chooses yes or no to Talks. Two yes: Talks.
3. Talks that was already in force at the previous Propaganda round, with no breach since: resolve section 7.4.
4. Either faction may leave Talks (to Reciprocal Restraint) or Reciprocal Restraint (to None) at this step. The State pays no cost. The PKK pays Europe -1 if it leaves Reciprocal Restraint for None (step 1 rule).

### 7.4 Negotiated Outcome

Talks never give an instant win (DD-11.3). A Negotiated Outcome needs all of:

1. Talks held through a full Campaign with no breach.
2. Legal Space is not Banned.
3. Both factions accept these concessions: the State sets Emergency Rule to Normalizing and Legal Space to Open; the PKK removes half its domestic Guerrillas, round up, to Available.

If both accept, apply the concessions. The game ends. Margins decide which faction negotiated from strength (09, section 5). If either refuses, Talks continues or ends at that faction's choice. Evidence: none of the historical ceasefires was recognized by the State [F-039]; the PKK shifted toward a settlement inside Turkey [F-037; E-0207]. Basis: Sol V-B, "Negotiation as an outcome".

## 10. Kurdish Hizbullah and covert violence

### 10.1 Kurdish Hizbullah

Event-driven only. No player owns or orders it [M-016; F-058; F-057].

1. Card titles are neutral, for example "Unattributed killings in the southeast".
2. Effect: if any of Botan, Mardin-Batman, Diyarbakır City and Diyarbakır Rural holds an Office, remove one Office from the highest-Pop such space. Otherwise remove one Guerrilla from the highest-Pop such space with a Guerrilla. Ties go in this order: Mardin-Batman, Diyarbakır City, Diyarbakır Rural, Botan. The executing faction does not choose the target. Place a Disputed Harm marker there.
3. Attribution limit: a later event may turn a Disputed marker into a State Harm marker only if the card rests on a High or Moderate court or commission finding. Reported security-force links rest on a 1995 commission as relayed by HRW [F-058; E-0087]. No court has settled them.
4. Never print the "2,000 murders" figure [Q-403].

### 10.2 Covert violence

1. Covert Action is the only player tool for covert violence (06, section 3.5). Its markers are hidden, revealable and costly [M-009].
2. Reveal events: Susurluk [E-0015], commission reports [E-0109; E-0223], each revealing up to 2 Covert markers chosen at random.
3. Killings of legal-party and press figures with disputed perpetrators appear only as events with Disputed Harm markers and role names, never names [F-019; E-0098; E-0189; E-0193].
4. Court outcomes were mixed: convictions for the Susurluk gang and Agar, acquittals in JITEM cases [F-028; E-0242; E-0248; E-0249; E-0250]. Card text keeps that mix.
