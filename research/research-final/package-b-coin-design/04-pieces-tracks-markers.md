# 04 Pieces, Tracks and Markers

Status: rules draft. Every count, range and start value is a design value. Values marked [tune] need playtest calibration. No piece count is a historical force figure (DD-15.2; M-028). Scenario start values are in 10.

## 1. Scale

A piece is a unit of weight, not a unit of people. Source strength figures set only the order of magnitude, and all of them have Low or Moderate confidence:

1. State forces: about 60,000 regular gendarmerie in 1992, about 160,000 troops and gendarmerie in early 1994, about 220,000 troops by 1997 [Q-305; Q-304; Q-357].
2. Village Guards: about 7,933 in 1985, 20,000 in Feb 1992, 45,000 in 1994, 67,000 in Jun 1995. A peak of 90,000 to 95,000 has no year [Q-184; Q-180; Q-344; Q-183; Q-186; F-048].
3. PKK fighters: about 10,000 in 1992, 7,000 to 10,000 in late 1993, about 11,000 at peak, 1,500 inside Turkey in summer 1999 per Turkish officers [Q-297; Q-298; Q-300; Q-301].

The inventory gives the State about twice as many pieces as the PKK. Real ratios were larger. Concealment and sanctuary balance the difference (CT 1.4.3).

### 2.1 Piece rules

1. Pieces enter play from Available only, unless an event says otherwise (CT 1.4.1). If a needed type is not Available, the owner may take it from the map, except Guards and pieces in external boxes.
2. New Guerrillas are always placed Underground (CT 1.4.3). Underground Guerrillas cannot be removed by Assault (06, section 2.4).
3. A removed piece returns to its owner's Available box. There is no Casualties box [tune]. CT uses Casualties (CT 6.5); this design drops it to cut moving parts.
4. Village Guards start the campaign Out of Play. The Law 3175 event moves all 16 to Available [E-0004; E-0005]. Before that event, no rule or card may place a Guard (DD-08.1).
5. A removed Guard goes to Available. It never becomes a PKK piece (DD-08.4; CT 4.3.2 Subvert rejected).
6. Bases never move. Troops, Police and Guerrillas move by operation, special activity, Redeploy or event. Guards never move (DD-08.2).

### 3.1 Resources

Range 0 to 50 (CT 1.8). A faction that must pay more than it has pays to 0. A faction that cannot pay an action's cost cannot take it (CT 1.8). Resources are a design currency. The research does not support an income model [Q-359 is Low and is not used].

### 3.2 Legitimacy

One national State track, 0 to 20 (DD-06.3). It records domestic and international acceptance of State conduct, not support in a space. It feeds the State victory score (DD-14.2), State income (05, section 6.2) and the Civic Action spending cap (05, section 6.6). It never falls below 0 and never rises above 20. A Legitimacy loss that the track cannot pay in full costs the State 2 Resources per point not paid; if the State cannot pay, the rest is lost [tune]. Reason: at 0 the bans, breaches and reveals otherwise cost nothing (11, section 6; IR-35). Sources and drains are in 07, section 2 [M-004; M-024; F-049].

### 3.3 Emergency Rule

Four steps, printed in this order: Martial Law, OHAL, OHAL Extended Powers, Normalizing. Each step has its own drain and unlocks (07, section 8). The track does not use "higher" and "lower" except as the table in 07 defines [M-010; F-013; E-0055].

### 3.4 Host postures

Three separate tracks (DD-09.1). Syria has four steps. KDP and PUK add "Aligned with Turkey" beyond Hostile (DD-09.2). Each posture step changes PKK Rally, March, income and State Cross-Border access (07, section 5). No posture resets by itself (DD-09.4).

### 3.5 Ceasefire

Four steps (DD-11.1). Only the PKK moves it from None to Declared (DD-11.2). Details in 07, section 7.

### 3.6 Europe

Range 0 to 5. The Europe track measures diaspora resources and international pressure on both factions. It is not a Turkish political will track (CT lessons, "Two tempting borrowings to reject").

Absent in C1 and C2 (DD-10.1). The HEP event at the start of C3 sets it to Open [E-0007; O-030]. Closure cases move it to Prosecuted, bans move it to Banned and successors return it to Prosecuted (07, section 6) [F-015; F-052; M-007].

### 4.1 Support and Opposition levels

Each populated domestic space is at one of five levels: Active Support, Passive Support, Neutral, Passive Opposition, Active Opposition (DD-06.2). Neutral has no marker. A shift moves one level. A shift from Passive Support toward Opposition goes to Neutral first.

Total Support = Pop of Passive Support spaces + 2 x Pop of Active Support spaces.
Total Opposition = Pop of Passive Opposition spaces + 2 x Pop of Active Opposition spaces.

Maximum Total Support or Total Opposition: 2 x 32 = 64. The double count for Active levels is a design choice that follows the five-level scale in DD-06. CT uses three levels (CT 1.6).

### 4.2 Harm count

State Harm count = State Harm markers on the map.
PKK Harm count = PKK Harm markers on the map + Coercion markers on the map.

Coercion counts for the PKK because DD-14.3 puts a Harm gate on its own coercion. Disputed markers count for neither (Claude harm file, section 4, rule 7). Use in 09.

## 6. Tests

1. Count pieces left in Available at the end of each Campaign. If either side runs out of a piece type in more than one game in three, raise the count.
2. Check that the five-level scale does not make Active levels the only goal. Compare scores with Active counted once.
3. Check that the Harm count stays readable. Two readers must compute it alike from the board.
