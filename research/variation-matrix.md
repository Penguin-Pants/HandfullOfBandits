# COIN variation matrix

For each mechanic: which games use it and how it differs. Source: `analysis/games/*.md` (rulebook sections cited there). Codes as in `common-system.md`. Extra codes: FOS Fall of Saigon, SOD Sovereign of Discord, ARIO Ariovistus, EOME Enemy of My Enemy (Arab Revolt). BW covers Palestine, Malaya, Kenya and Cyprus unless a name is given. "n/v" means not verified in the game summary. Rows marked "inferred" are derived by comparison.

## 2. Turn order

| Mechanic | Games | Variation |
|---|---|---|
| Faction order on card, 1st and 2nd Eligible | AA, CL, ADP, FITL, LOD, FS, PDG, GAN, CW, RDR | Standard. Order is the symbol row on the card |
| Initiative Track (three boxes) | CT, BW, EOME | Two players. Boxes: Event, Limited Op, Op plus Special Activity (plus Pass). Left-most box acts first next card |
| Three Factions act on one card | PP, ABB | PP: 3rd Eligible takes Limited Op or Pass. Limited Op keeps Eligibility. ABB: no order printed on cards, order set by actions |
| Look-ahead card | AA, CL, ADP, FITL, LOD, FS, PDG, GAN, ABB (2 shown), RDR (2 shown) | CT removes it. PP hides the next card |
| Pass reward | all | AA +1, Govt +3. CL +1, Govt +3. ADP +1, COIN Pass gives Govt +3. FITL +1 or ARVN +3. LOD +1 Patriots and Indians, +2 British and French. FS +1 Gaul, +2 Roman. CT +1 FLN, +2 Govt. PDG Britons +3, Barbarians +1 Renown. GAN Raj +3, Rev +1, NV move an Activist. ABB +1. PP +1, Govt +3. RDR MG +3, CORP activate Cycler, RD +1, CR draw Asset. CW JPN +3, NAT +2, WLD and CPC +1. BW draw chit, Rob, Ambush, shift Pipeline, or Resources |
| Restriction before every scoring round | FITL, FS | FITL Monsoon: no Sweep or March, Air limited, no Pivotal. FS Frost: no March |
| Restriction when a Dust Storm card is next (not Monsoon or Frost) | RDR | Haboob blocks Recon and March; Reclaimers may still Travel (RDR 4.0) |
| Restriction before the final scoring round only | AA, CL, ADP | Player Operations limited. AA and ADP also bar Sweep and March (AA 2.3.9, CL 2.3.9, ADP 2.3.8) |
| Sudden scoring option | CL, ADP, FS, PDG (Epoch swap), LOD (Winter is Coming), GAN (Campaign swap) | Swap the two showing cards and run the round now |
| Pivotal, Brilliant Stroke | FITL, CT, FOS, LOD, PDG | Faction holds a card that cancels an Event. Trump order set by rule (FITL 2.3.8, LOD 2.3.8, PDG 2.3.8) |
| Purchased initiative | RDR | Church of the Reclaimer discards Asset cards to move up (RDR 4.1) |
| Extra tempo tools | CL Launder, AA Ship, CW Large Offensive, PP Operation Only declaration | Free Limited Op or full Op |

## 3. Victory

| Game | Faction formulas (threshold) | Check timing |
|---|---|---|
| AA | Govt Support over 60. FARC Opposition + Bases over 25. AUC Bases over FARC Bases. Cartels Bases over 10 and Resources over 40 | Propaganda Round |
| CL | Govt all Cities Active Support and Support over 18. 26July Opp + Bases over 15. DR Controlled Pop + Bases over 9. Syndicate open Casinos over 7 and Resources over 30 | Propaganda Round |
| ADP | Coalition Support + Available pieces over 30. Govt COIN Pop + Patronage over 35. Taliban Opp + Bases over 20. Warlords uncontrolled Pop over 15 and Resources over 40 | Propaganda Round |
| FITL | US Support + Available over 50. ARVN COIN Pop + Patronage over 50. NVA Controlled Pop + Bases over 18. VC Opp + Bases over 35 | Coup Round |
| LOD | Two conditions per Faction (Support minus Opposition over 10, CBC against CRC, Forts against Villages). Summed at game end | Winter Quarters |
| FS | Romans Subdued + Dispersed + Allies over 15. Arverni off-map Legions over 6 and Allies + Citadels over 8. Aedui Allies + Citadels above every other Faction. Belgae Control Value + Allies + Citadels over 15 | Winter, Victory Phase |
| CT | Govt Support + Commitment over 35. FLN Opposition + Bases over 30 | Propaganda Round, not the first |
| PDG | Dux Prosperity + Prestige, Civitates Briton Control, Saxons Renown and Settlements, Scotti Renown and Settlements. Thresholds change with Imperium status | Victory Phase |
| GAN | Raj Support + Pop over 38. Congress Opposition over 20. League Muslim Opposition over 14. Revolutionaries Unrest Pop + Bases over 14 | Campaign card |
| ABB | Reds Opposition + Administrations over 11 with Polarization limit. Senate Town Pop over 3 with limit. Moderates Resources over 14 and Issues limit | Propaganda Round |
| PP | Govt Controlled Pop + Patronage over 18. Reformers Opposition Pop + Bases over 12. NPA Resistance Pop + Bases over 13 | Election Round |
| RDR | MG Support + EG Confidence over 34. CORP Profits over 36. RD Opposition + Bases over 32. CR Control spaces + Bases over enemy Bases | Dust Storm Round |
| CW | JPN LoC Econ + Commitment over 40. NAT Support Pop + Patronage over 40. WLD Controlled Pop + Bases over 25. CPC Opposition + Bases over 20 | Propaganda Round |
| BW | One shared British Political Will track. Insurgent wins at 0 (Malaya and Kenya also at 2 or lower). British win at the end or at 18 or more | Propaganda Round |

Common points:

- Margin = score minus threshold for single-threshold Factions. Multi-part conditions use their own rule (FS Arverni: lower of two margins; AA Cartels, CL Syndicate, ADP Warlords: lower of two). Relative conditions use a comparison (FS Aedui: own total minus the best other Faction; AA AUC; RDR Church). If no one wins at the end, the highest margin wins (games with a margin formula).
- Two-part conditions: AA Cartels, CL Govt and Syndicate, ADP Warlords, FS Arverni, ABB, LOD.
- Relative conditions: AA AUC, FS Aedui, RDR Church.
- Total Support counts: AA, CL, FITL, LOD use 2 x Active + 1 x Passive.
- Available forces count for the foreign supporter: ADP Coalition, FITL US (withdrawal scores).
- Shared tracks as victory (inferred): BW only.

## 11. Unique mechanics (one line each)

- AA: Shipments (traded drugs), FARC Zones, El Presidente track.
- CL: Casinos, Cash and Launder, US Alliance, Skim.
- ADP: Coalition with no Resources, Returnees, Surge, Pashtun spaces, Casualties cut Aid.
- FITL: Trail, Pivotal trump chain, NVA conventional Troops, RVN Leader cards.
- FOS and SOD: Armor, Paris Peace Talks, Posture tracks, Cadres, Strategic Hamlets.
- LOD: Treaty of Alliance, Brilliant Stroke, Blockades, Leaders.
- FS: Leader proximity, Battle steps, Dispersal, Senate track, Germans, negotiated Supply Line and Retreat.
- ARIO: Settlements, Intimidate, Diviciacus, Interlude.
- CT: two-player Initiative Track, Pivotal chain, France Track, Resettlement, hidden Deception markers.
- PDG: Imperium, Raiders, Prosperity and Plunder, Foederati.
- GAN: nonviolent Factions, Gandhi leader piece, Restraint and Unity, Crisis, Jail.
- ABB: reversed Active meaning, Vassalage, Polarization, Phase I and II.
- PP: triangle of three Factions, Reformers, Personalities.
- RDR: Asset hand, Aldrin Cycler, moving Population, Dust Storms.
- CW: four conventional and guerrilla actors, LoC victory, Large Offensive, Disaster and Glorious Victory.
- BW: single Political Will track, Intel Chits, Prison, Haganah, Pipeline, Commander, International Opinion.
